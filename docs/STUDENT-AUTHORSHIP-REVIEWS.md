# Exact-work student authorship from a university identifier

An optional Cambridge `reviewed/student-authorship-review.json` certifies one
retained member on one already accepted lab paper. It leaves all ordinary
name-only/source-record guards intact. It admits no student or paper, changes no
name, school, role, date, source observation or adviser relationship, and supplies
no collection/OpenAlex/global-person identity anchor.

A fresh independent reviewer must replay original source bytes and exact quotes.
The original PhD supervisor directory must bind the named student, source record
and owned university profile to the specific CRSid. A captured university policy
must explicitly support the literal `CRSid@cam.ac.uk` mailbox convention. Version
1 supports that domain only; departmental and other email aliases are not inferred.

The complete primary PDF must establish its exact DOI, title, publication date,
complete ordered author list, and the member's unambiguous named author slot,
literal university mailbox and explicit University of Cambridge affiliation.
An address in references, acknowledgements or another person's correspondence
proves nothing. The source bytes, complete text hash, parser result and page count
are retained. A publisher DOI or OA cluster's author ORCID alone is insufficient.
Version1 holds nonempty edition groups until their authorships have separate proof.

The stated enrollment/end or current-capture interval establishes the person's
Cambridge PhD student interval, never a PI advising start. Publication dates at
stated precision must fit that interval. The separately labelled historical
student route implements the existing six-year post-degree link window; it
claims former-student authorship, never publication-time enrollment. Future,
undated, competing and uncertain identities or times remain held.

The decision binds every scientific member, accepted-paper and PI field, except
explicit AtlasID augmentation and build summary counters. A new native source,
member relation, paper metadata/edition or PI claim makes the proof stale.
Duplicate decisions never use first-row wins. Existing links remain unchanged.

A certified link carries `institutionalAuthorshipReview.identity_scope=exact_work`.
Both AtlasIDs byline bridge paths exclude these proofs through the common
`work_only` predicate. Complete pre-ID and post-ID validation rechecks the
scientific preimages and typed proof. This does not repair or authorize any old
global join. The absent optional input preserves ordinary behavior.

The consumer runs after ordinary weak-member filtering and honors active
student-link exclusions. Its input limit is 5 MiB. It records every applied or
held decision in `report.json` as `studentAuthorshipReview`. Scientific
preimages also bind the complete native work source and its ordered byline;
competing native versions are held.

Original directory HTML, university mail-policy HTML and primary PDF bytes must
be available in the private content-addressed `raw/source-evidence/<sha256>.bin`
cache. Input rows cannot choose filesystem paths. Consumption rehashes original
bytes, replays the source-owned directory entry and PDF text, rejects duplicate
native IDs, and checks quotes against the actual publication header. Neither raw
source bodies nor that private cache are committed with reviewed inputs.

A separately labelled `recorded_student_publication_window` implements
R-LNK-01/03's two years before to six after retained student years. Version1
requires those years to belong to this exact original PhD directory relation
and agree with its stated end/current observation year. It derives both bounds
from the bound native member, never accepts input-supplied window bounds, and
requires complete native and primary publication-date intervals inside them.
It certifies authorship by a recorded student; it makes no publication-time
enrollment, degree-end or PI advising-start claim. Title footnote markers may
trail a complete title; words, title substrings and unrelated editions may not.
