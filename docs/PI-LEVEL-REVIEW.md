# PI status and lab coverage review

Review each collected lab in the 2016–2026 school window. Keep three conclusions separate: whether the person is an eligible PI, which appointment dates are supported, and how completely the lab's students and papers have been collected. Confirmed PI status does not make an empty roster or bibliography complete.

## Confirm the PI and appointment

Follow the person's actual profile from the official faculty directory. Retain the directory entry, individual profile, literal role, unit, original capture, hash, and observation date. Check independent research responsibility through a school group page naming its PI, an explicit advising statement, or a current faculty profile with research and recruitment responsibilities. An assistant professor can qualify before a first student or school paper appears. A postdoc role, collaborator byline, incoming appointment, or a lab name alone cannot establish a current independent PI.

Verify name order and aliases against these sources. Bind bibliographic identifiers through an official link, reciprocal owned URL, or exact owned bibliography matches. Inspect disambiguation and conflicting affiliations; do not bind an ambiguous unsuffixed author record by name. Preserve stable person identifiers and distinguish shared groups from each PI's own lab.

Record current rank separately from historical appointments. Preserve exact dates, seasons, years, and first confirmed dates at their actual precision. A capture date is not an employment start. A current appointment cannot supply earlier faculty years. Honorary, visiting, postdoc, and primary appointments remain distinct under the existing admission rules.

## Reconcile the data before checking an item

For students, inspect current and alumni sections on the actual profile and officially discovered sites. Follow named students' own pages, thesis records, and dated official announcements where useful. Record the literal role, years, and whether the source establishes advising or lab membership. Coauthorship alone supplies neither. A recruiting notice supports recruitment activity; it does not prove a zero roster.

For papers, reconcile the full owned bibliography, official publication lists, the retained identifier sources, and collected works. Give every citation a disposition: admitted, another edition of an admitted work, outside the school appointment, unrelated person, excluded genre, or unresolved. Retain source dates and dump cutoffs. Distinguish publisher first online dates from copyright years, repository dates, and database creation dates. Follow current author links when an old PDF URL fails. Respect host restrictions; use permitted publisher or author originals without bypassing access controls.

For every admitted work, account for a verified local copy or its specific access or collection status. Distinguish a retained original from a catalog entry and a missing download from demonstrated closed access. Check student-paper links through the ordinary role, identity, byline, affiliation, and date rules.

## Record conclusions and followups

Use separate fields for `pi_status`, `appointment_status`, and `coverage_status`. PI status can be `confirmed`, `excluded`, or `unresolved`. Each data dimension must state its measured count, measurement scope, sources, limits, and next action when needed. An unmeasured count stays unknown. A native zero means zero admitted records in that build, not zero real students or publications.

Use `coverage_status: needs_followup` when a known qualifying roster or paper is missing, a candidate has unresolved affiliation or date evidence, or a material source remains unchecked. Keep exact unresolved records and next actions visible in the queue. A reason explains a gap; it does not automatically justify closing it. A bounded absence can be checked only after the relevant sources have been inspected and the reason states what they establish.

After corrections, rerun the ordinary native stages with unchanged admission rules. Retain the full school identity exclusions when using an isolated PI run, and label its scope. Reconcile source decisions with the resulting members, papers, links, and copy accounting before updating the inventory. Preserve the original inventory snapshot, prior reports, and superseded reasons. Reopen a completed review when new contradictory evidence appears. Separate PI source review from independent accuracy certification and school package promotion.
