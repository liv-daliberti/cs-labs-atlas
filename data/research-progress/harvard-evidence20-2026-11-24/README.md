The rebuilt native Harvard package has **212 labs, 714 qualifying student–lab records, and 918 all member–lab records**. These include current students and alumni during 2016–2026; they are not unique-person or current-enrolment counts. It has **93 labs with no qualifying student recorded** and **14 with one**. Missing evidence does not prove that a real-world lab has no students.

Shared REU students retain independently supported memberships under both mentors. Postdocs, staff, committee-only ties, coauthors and undated historical roles were not converted into students.
