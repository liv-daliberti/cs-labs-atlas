01

A fixed university cohort, computer science labs with papers

The 2025 Academic Ranking of World Universities, through rank 300. Published rank bands are retained. At Princeton, CMU, EPFL, MIT, Berkeley and Stanford the atlas shows the computing departments’ PI labs, one per faculty PI, as the CS departments page lists them, when the lab has at least one lab paper; PI labs without lab papers are not shown on either page. At every other university it shows the computer science units (field computer and information sciences, or a computing department such as computer science, EECS, informatics, robotics, software, data science or AI) that have at least one linked paper. Units without papers, other fields and the earlier inventory of these six schools remain in the collection and are not shown; each university lists how many are hidden.

02

Named, traceable research units

Labs, groups, centers, institutes and chairs come from official institutional sources. An undated directory listing establishes a candidate unit, not its active years. Units with a sourced department or school appear in that group. Other units remain directly browsable under their university while affiliations are checked. In this computer science view, a lab appears under its computing department; a computer-science lab housed elsewhere appears under its own department.

03

Two publication views

PI as last author: a verified author identity is last on the work and that authorship lists the university. This is a PI-based proxy. Reviewed lab bibliography: an explicit lab bibliography supports the link, regardless of author order. Exact lab-listed arXiv preprints with a verified PI and matching complete bylines can enter this reviewed view when affiliation metadata is missing. This confirms a bibliography listing, not historical lab tenure. The combined view is their union. These sets may overlap; none is exhaustive.

04

History without invented lifespans

Dated evidence supports its stated year or event interval. Years between observations stay unknown. PI moves, leadership changes, nested centers, renamed labs, and author-order differences need review.
