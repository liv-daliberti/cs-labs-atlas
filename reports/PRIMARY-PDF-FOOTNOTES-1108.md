# Complete primary-PDF affiliation reviews, version 2

An ordered OpenAlex byline can use `Family, Given` while its PDF prints `Given Family`, and several authors can share one printed institutional line. The original complete-block reviewer correctly held those layouts: it could neither find the raw comma names in the PDF nor spend the same institutional words repeatedly. Version2 adds explicit complete shared-footnote and inline reviews. Version1 takes its original unchanged validation path.

The new reviewer retains the full original-work, original-authorships, ordered raw byline/author-ID, PDF URL/hash, complete first-page text/hash, reviewer and timestamp bindings. It accepts only literal name spellings or an explicit single-comma family/given formatting conversion. It does not join nicknames, initials, near spellings, or transliterations.

For `layout="footnotes"`, `authors` gives every ordered original author with the PDF spelling, literal name-plus-marker quote, name/marker bounding box, ordered marker tokens and affiliation-group IDs. `groups` gives each unique printed affiliation marker, complete literal text/quote and bounding box. An optional unmarked default group applies only to authors without an affiliation marker. The declared group IDs must exactly match the author's printed markers. Correspondence and equal-contribution notes are explicit separate marker declarations; they cannot replace a printed affiliation marker. Numeric10 is one marker, not1+0; letter-marker case is significant.

Shared institutional text is counted once. A complete header partition binds title/publication notice or one actual warning line, the complete ordered printed byline, all affiliation-group lines and a plain email contact tail. Undefined/duplicate/conflicting/unused markers, omitted or extra authors, incomplete groups, source-word overuse, overlapping or invalid coordinates, and altered source bindings hold the entire review. No partial substitution occurs. The correspondence-envelope glyph decoded as `B` in one real PDF requires an explicit visual extraction attestation, literal contact email, and exactly one corresponding marked author; it is not a general name or marker alias.

For `layout="inline"`, every author retains a complete contiguous printed name/affiliation statement and nonoverlapping source box. Only the literal PDF spelling or comma-format conversion changes; the original-block completeness and word-budget guards remain. Layouts without an unambiguous bound title/header, unsupported marker legends, or non-email contact tails are conservatively held. Opaque Unicode names/titles remain literal; there is no ASCII-empty title key or generated reading.

`school_flags` continues to apply the existing charter/campus predicate to a deep-copied derived view only. Original records, raw author IDs/names/affiliations, PI eligibility, person identity and verified/candidate link statuses do not change. The full native fixtures show an existing candidate can gain the school-affiliation basis while remaining a candidate, and invalid/unrequested reviews preserve existing fallback behavior.
