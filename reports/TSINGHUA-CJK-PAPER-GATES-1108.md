# Literal CJK paper candidate and title gates

Two losses were confirmed before the first Tsinghua native paper trial. `Name` already recognizes exact opaque CJK names, but the census candidate index contained only first/family forms and `identity.hits` skipped one-token bylines. Separately, ASCII-only title keys erased pure Chinese titles and collapsed mixed Chinese titles sharing a Latin acronym. Empty collection/title-identity keys could also create unsupported matches.

The fix indexes `Name.mono` separately and uses that exact index for the normal and page-1 candidate routes. It does not split Chinese names, guess a family name, transliterate, or generate bilingual aliases. The existing reviewed identity, PI-own school evidence, appointment/window and type checks remain required. Different opaque names remain distinct in reviewed coauthor-cluster checks. An explicit bilingual label still uses its supplied Latin reading, demonstrated with the existing diagnostic label `Song GUO 郭嵩` and byline `Song Guo`; no HKUST input is written.

Title normalization keeps literal nonLatin letters/digits, preserving distinct Chinese titles and mixed titles sharing `AI`. The historical Latin/ASCII fold remains exact:622 individual characters plus accented/ligature/modifier examples checked without a mismatch. Distinct Cyrillic/Greek letters and nonLatin digits remain distinct. Empty keys cannot match collection works or establish title-based identity; DOI matching remains available. The same Chinese title can group genuine editions while retaining each edition's full byline. Distinct Chinese or mixed-acronym titles remain separate.
