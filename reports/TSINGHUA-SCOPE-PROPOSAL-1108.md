The fix extends the existing charter-aware related-institution gate to `openalex_excluded`, on a deep copy of each work. An excluded entry loses only its borrowed primary-school lineage. A separate direct primary entry, or this authorship's school-naming raw affiliation, can still support school attribution; explicit clauses naming another author remain rejected. An excluded child-only PI cannot regain school evidence through a school coauthor or the cross-affiliation fallback. The existing documented joint-appointment exception applies to related entries, never to excluded entries. No institution IDs are hardcoded in implementation.

The same gate covers the normal sweep and outside-sweep `pi-works.sqlite` reader. Identity anchors may establish the person independently, but do not waive own-school scope. Recovery collectors now preserve source authorships for replay; the native recovered-row reader reapplies the charter to this PI's authorship. Legacy OpenAlex recoveries holding only display strings remain held when no matching native source is available. Independent primary page-1 evidence retains its existing route. Original source metadata and canonical files remain unchanged.
