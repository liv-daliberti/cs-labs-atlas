# Georgia Tech: another 25 empty labs

77 individually reviewed student relationships in 25 labs that previously had zero recorded students. Every selected lab gains at least two students. The expected native totals are 74 zero-student labs, 60 one-student labs, and 1,984 eligible student memberships (baseline 99 / 60 / 1,907).

The evidence combines original university dissertations and commencement programs, faculty and student CVs, and faculty-owned lab rosters. Each record preserves original response-byte SHA256, literal quotations, explicit role and relationship type, observation years, and corroborating sources. Original bytes are retained at the paths in sources.json. The existing native builder is unchanged.

Conservative decisions: lab membership does not imply formal advising; committee membership does not imply advising, while explicitly identified dissertation co-chairs count. Expected graduation is not degree completion. MID-only entries retain a generic student role. Other-university rosters after a PI move, undated historical alumni, pre-2016 observations, collaborator-only entries, and inaccessible/robots-excluded sources remain held. The mixed Sinha roster is limited to independently confirmed Georgia Tech students. Michael Nistche / Natasia Boland source typos are independently resolved to Nitsche / Natashia Boland without treating coauthorship as relationship evidence.

`prepare_batch.py` replays reviewed-entries.json, verifies original source hashes and literal quotes, checks the reviewed schema, and writes additions.json and projection.json. `audit_pdf_positions.py` independently checks program candidate/adviser column positions. The shared `../gatech-students10-1126/validate_batch.py` validates all eight recovery batches against the actual native package, including names, roles, source evidence, years, relationship types, counts, and preservation of other labs.

The private native build must preserve all 60 singleton-lab identities and remove exactly these 25 labs from the zero-student set. Canonical promotion requires a fully tested landed commit, exact imported-builder equivalence, unchanged canonical input hashes, source equality, schema validation, atomic replacement with backups, and a refreshed global gap export.
