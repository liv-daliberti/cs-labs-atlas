# Ten additional UIUC labs: students and papers

This batch continues after `73eb24c2`, with ten additional labs explicitly authorized by the owner. It preserves that batch's canonical baseline: 2,079 qualifying student–lab memberships, 127 labs with students, 5,609 lab papers and 2,313 student–paper links.

Full available student/current/alumni/advising sources support 159 research observations for 152 distinct student–lab memberships in the selected ten labs; two memberships already existed. The separate unresolved register retains 119 observations rather than inventing degree types, dates, aliases, or prior-campus continuation. Source completeness is limited to the available reviewed public sources; this is not an exhaustive census of all actual students or all 370 UIUC labs.

Official Gray Books restore 62 point appointment-year observations. Each retained original PDF is hashed and its printed date, campus, name, home unit and regular faculty rank checked independently. These are individual annual observations, not inferred continuous tenure. All prior reviewed groups, appointment proofs, native paper identities, students, relations, sources and links must survive the final build.

The paper track accounts for full captured publication lists rather than fixed-size combined searches. MRFIL's captured site has no publication list; linked NCBI/experts pages are access-limited and were not bypassed. The native catalogue is still rebuilt for Sutton using additional official appointment years. Other unavailable or unresolved source entries remain visible with reasons.

The rebuilt package is promoted only after independent source, appointment, publication, paper-acceptance and native audits, all five schema checks, exact old-record preservation checks, and the full repository landing gate. The authorized push is local `main` to remote `dev`; release publication is separate.
