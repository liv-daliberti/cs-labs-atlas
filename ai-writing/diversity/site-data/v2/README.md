# Site data v2 for the AI-writing pages (owner 2026-11-04: the four upgrades)

Written by `ai-writing/dose/ai_pages.py assemble` from the stage tables in `ai-writing/data/ai-scores/results/ai_pages/` (git-ignored); the PNGs for the report are in
`ai-writing/diversity/reports/figures-ai-pages/`. Stages: `frame`, `umap`, `labs`, `matched`, `fig3` on Slurm (`ai_pages.slurm`, CPU), then `assemble`. Plain JSON, `null` = not available.
**No 2026 anywhere** (a partial year): series end at 2025, **pre = 2016-2022, post = 2023-2025**, the placebo window is 2017-2021 (`tests/test_ai_pages_data.py`).

Vocabulary used throughout. **dose** = the share of a paper's body passages flagged by the passage detector (`ai_share_cal` where a school or field needed a recut, else `ai_share`; the same `x` as the dose pages).
**AI paper** = dose >= 25% (the dose pages' default cutoff); only full-text papers have a dose. **abstract flag** (`abf`) = the v1 abstract detector flags the abstract (all papers with an abstract score).
Embedding model keys: `specter2_proximity` (headline), `specter2_base`, `scincl`, `qwen3_0p6b`, `gte_qwen2_1p5b`, `bge_m3`, `nomic_v15`. Vectors are the abstract vectors centred on the corpus mean and length-normalised.

## Ids (the lab_id mismatch)
- **Paper id** = the universe `paper_id` = the **atlas work id `atl:w:...`** for the seven released schools: exactly a `lookup_keys` entry of the release reader rows (`releases/<id>/reader/<school>.jsonl.gz`; the site's reader and `cs-hierarchy/<school>.json.gz` `atlasId` use it). All nine schools are in every file (owner 2026-11-05). Georgia Tech and UChicago are unreleased: `map_index.json` has `released {school: bool}`, every lab record has `released`, their `site_lab` is `null` and their papers are not in the release reader (`oa:W...` ids), so show them but do not link them.
- **Lab id**: the dose pages' lab files use the universe id `atl:l:<school>:<pi-slug>`; the site's hierarchy pages use `lab_cs_pi_...` / `lab_pi_...` (`atlas/data/cs-hierarchy/<school>.json.gz` `labs[].id`). They are not the same string. Every record here carries **both**: `lab_id` (universe, joins `labs_ai_hist_<school>.json`) and **`site_lab`** (the hierarchy id, link target). The join is (school, PI name) between the universe's `lab_papers` and the site lab's `pi.name`; it is unique at all seven released schools, and `frame` checks that the joined labs' 2016-2025 papers are the same set (overlap 1.00 for every joined lab).

## 1. Paper map
| file | content |
|---|---|
| `map_index.json` | `meta` (model, projection recipe, `grid` 4096, `coord_range` of the UMAP axes, per-school counts `shards`, field semantics, `sizes` of every map file), `schools`, `fields` (OpenAlex primary fields), `topics` [{id, label, kind (`llm`/`ml`/`other`), n}], `labs` [{id, site, pi, name, school}] |
| `map_<school>.json.gz` | one shard per school (all nine) (a paper is in its home school's shard), struct of arrays, all of length `n`, sorted by year then id: `ids` (atlas id), `x`, `y` (integers 0..4095; `x_umap = x_min + (x + 0.5)/4096 * (x_max - x_min)`), `year` (offset from 2016), `lab` (index into `labs`, the paper's first lab), `topic` (index into `topics`, -1 none), `field` (index into `fields`, -1 none), `ai` (dose in percent 0..100, -1 = no usable full text), `abf` (1/0 abstract flag, -1 none) |
| `map_titles_<school>.json.gz` | `{school, n, titles}`, `titles[i]` belongs to row i of the shard (200 characters at most) |
| `map_density.json` | `grid` 64, `range`, `years.<year>`, `pre` (2016-2022), `post` (2023-2025): `n` and `ai` as sparse `[cell, count]` lists (cell = row * 64 + col; col from x, row from y); `ai` counts papers with dose >= 25% |
Projection: PCA(50) of the proximity vectors, then UMAP (n_neighbors 15, min_dist 0.1, euclidean, `random_state` 20261104), **fitted once on all papers** 2016-2025 with an abstract vector. Topics are BERTopic topics of the same vectors (outliers reassigned to the nearest topic, as `EMBEDDINGS-TOPICS-NOVELTY.md`).

## 2. Lab scatter: `lab_scatter.json.gz`
`meta` (definitions), `labs` [{lab_id, site_lab, pi, lab, school, n_pre, n_post, m, n_scored_post, dose, share25, abs_flag_share, values}], `slopes` [{measure, model, beta, lo, hi, labs, line_a, line_b_per_pct}].
- One row per lab with at least 5 abstract-vector papers before (2016-2022) and after (2023-2025). `m` = min(n_pre, n_post, 80) is the draw size; every value is the mean over 100 random draws of m papers of the period (DESIGN-v2.md).
- `dose` = the lab's mean dose over its 2023-2025 full-text papers (0..1), `share25` = their share with dose >= 25%, both `null` when fewer than 5 are scored; `abs_flag_share` = share of 2023-2025 abstracts flagged.
- `values[measure][model] = [pre, post, change]`. Measures: `dispersion` (mean distance to the draw's centroid), `mpd` (mean pairwise cosine distance), `vendi`, `topic_entropy` (bits), `effective_topics` (2^entropy), `extent` (knowledge extent: largest distance to the centroid), `dist_field` (mean paper novelty against all earlier papers, k = 3), `new_topics` (share of papers whose topic is absent from the lab's papers of the three previous years). Topic partitions: BERTopic for `specter2_proximity` and `qwen3_0p6b`, 100 k-means clusters of the model's own vectors for the other five.
- `slopes`: standardized Huber slope (z-scored change on z-scored dose, controls log n_pre, log n_post, school dummies; as `lab_scatter_*.png` of the October battery), `lo`/`hi` = 95% percentile bootstrap over labs (500). `line_a + line_b_per_pct * dose%` is the raw-unit Huber line for drawing.

## 3. Where AI is used: `ai_use.json.gz`
`meta`, `topics` [{id, label, keywords, kind, n_papers, n[10], a[10], share[10], mean[10]}] (arrays over 2016..2025: scored papers, those with dose >= 25%, their share, mean dose; share/mean `null` below 5 papers), `fields` [{field, n_papers, n, a, share, mean}] (OpenAlex primary field, >= 50 papers),
`kind_series.{llm,ml,other,non_llm,all}` {n, a, share, lo, hi, mean} per year (interval = bootstrap over labs, 300 draws, null below 20 papers), `top_by_2025_share` and `top_by_change` (topics with >= 30 scored papers in both 2022 and 2025: share_2025, share_2022, change).
**LLM / ML marking**: `kind` of each BERTopic topic from its label and its 10 keywords by the regexes `LLM_RE` and `ML_RE` in `ai_pages.py` (documented in `meta.kind_rule`): `llm` when the label or at least 2 keywords match language-model terms, `ml` when the label or at least 3 keywords match machine-learning terms and it is not `llm`, else `other`. The page's "in vs outside LLM research" is `kind_series.llm` against `kind_series.non_llm`. `meta.llm_share_of_2025_*` give the share of LLM topics among 2025 papers and among 2025 AI papers.

## 4. Topic-matched test: `matched_test.json`, `event_study.json`
`matched_test.json`: `meta` (design, `populations`: post, placebo, placebo25, with years, papers, AI papers, threshold), `rows` [{population, model, measure, effect, lo, hi, raw, n_ai, n_non, n_ai_matched, cells, matched_cells, sd}].
- Cells = BERTopic topic x year x school. In every cell with both groups the difference of mean outcomes (AI minus non-AI), weighted by the cell's AI papers (the effect on the AI papers: coarsened exact matching). `raw` is the unmatched difference.
- `measure = novelty`: paper-level novelty (k = 3, all 7 models; negative effect = AI papers less novel); interval = cluster bootstrap over labs (first lab; 500 resamples). `dispersion`, `vendi`: size-matched sets of 150 AI and 150 non-AI papers with the same (topic, year, school) composition, mean of 200 draws, interval from 100 lab resamples x 10 draws.
- `wording` [{model, measure (dispersion, vendi), original, rewritten, share_wording, *_lo/_hi, ...}]: the size-matched comparison repeated on the all-AI-rewritten abstracts (same cells, cut and draws, same papers); `share_wording` = 1 - rewritten / original. Stays near the original = content; shrinks to 0 = wording.
- `population = post`: 2023-2025, AI = dose >= 25%. `placebo` (the rate-matched cut, owner-accepted): 2017-2021 with "AI" = the dose cut that flags as large a share of papers as the 25% cut does in 2023-2025 (those flags are detector noise). `placebo25`: 2017-2021 at the 25% cut itself (novelty only; very few papers).
`event_study.json`: `meta`, `models.<model>.<outcome>.<dose|abs_dose>` = {event [{year, beta, lo, hi}] for 2016-2020 and 2023-2025 (2021 base, 2022 dropped), estimates {did, placebo2019, placebo2020, placebo2021}, n, labs}; outcomes `disp`, `mpd`, `vendi`, `h_oa`, `h_bt` (2 models), `nov_all`, `nov_lab`. Shade the years up to 2022 (before ChatGPT, 30 Nov 2022). From `dose_lab.py did`.

## 5. Figure 3 for nine schools: `fig3_nine_schools.json`
`meta`, `groups` [{variant, school (all + 9), flagged, not_flagged, n_per_draw, extent {ai, not, ai_lower_share, diff_median}, entropy {...}}] with box statistics `{whislo, q1, med, q3, whishi, mean, n_outliers}` of 1,000 equal-size draws per group (`n = min(1000, 0.8 x the smaller group)`, groups under 30 are `skipped` with a reason, as `fig3.py`). `extent` = largest distance to the centroid; `entropy` in bits over 50 k-means clusters fitted on all 2023-2025 papers. Variant `ft25`: AI = dose >= 25% (full-text papers); variant `abstract`: AI = abstract flag (covers Georgia Tech). A school's group = every paper of its labs.

## 6. `headline.json`
`findings` (6): `{id, claim, values, number {value, lo, hi, unit, compare}, evidence {page, anchor, file, key}, cannot_conclude [...]}`. `claim` = `RENDER[id](values)` in `ai_pages_headline.py`; the test re-renders every claim. `evidence.page` is the site page slug (`how-much`, `given-ai`, `all-rewritten`); `anchor` is the element id the page should carry (`share-over-time`, `where-ai-is-used`, `topic-matched-test`, `lab-scatter`, `figure-3`).

## 7. AI-written vs not on every metric: `ai_vs_not.json[.gz]` (owner 2026-11-05, page "All papers AI-written")
Built by `ai-writing/dose/ai_vs_not.py` (`model --model M` per embedding model on Slurm, then `assemble`; sentences by `ai_vs_not_site.sentence`). Same slider as pages 1-2.
- **Groups.** For each cutoff t in `meta.ts` (1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 75, 100 % of passages flagged): *AI-written* = full-text papers with dose >= t; *not AI-written* = dose below min(t, 10%) (the detector-noise band; kept disjoint from the AI group, so for t < 10 the band is 0..t). Variant `abstract` (key `"0"`): papers without usable full text, by their abstract flag (1 vs 0), independent of t; the page shows it behind the page-1 toggle. Variant `placebo` (headline model, years 2016-2021 only): the rate-matched cut `meta.placebo_cut[t]` (flags as large a share of 2017-2021 papers as t does of 2023-2025), same not-band rule.
- **Cells.** `models.<model>.<variant>.<t>.<scope>` with scope `all` or one of the 9 schools (a school = every paper of its labs; `meta.released` says which are released: show Georgia Tech and UChicago, do not link them). `n` = `[[n_ai, n_not] per year 2016..2025]`; `m.<metric>.<version>` = `{gap, lo, hi}` arrays of 10 (years 2016..2025), `null` = blank (the smaller group has fewer than 20 papers; pre-2023 groups at high t are blank). Gap = AI-written minus not, mean over 40 equal-size draws of m = min(n_ai, n_not, 100) papers; interval = cluster bootstrap over labs (first lab; 24 resamples x 3 draws, lab multiplicities as sampling weights), 2.5/97.5 percentiles.
- **Metrics** (`meta.metrics`): `vendi`, `dispersion`, `mpd` (mean pairwise distance), `extent` (knowledge extent), `novelty` (paper-level mean, k = 3, against the previous 3 years, pool 2,000; computed separately on each version), `entropy_bt` (topic entropy over the BERTopic topics, bits), `entropy_oa` (over OpenAlex primary topics). The two entropies use fixed original topic labels, so they have no `rewritten` series (`wording_invariant`).
- **Versions.** `orig` = original abstract embeddings; `rewritten` = the same papers and the same draws on the all-AI-rewritten abstracts (every abstract rewritten by Qwen2.5-7B, polish; `allai/`), so wording is constant: a gap that stays is content, one that shrinks to 0 was wording. Only papers with a kept rewrite enter (both versions).
- **Models.** `meta.models` (the 7 embedding models, headline `specter2_proximity`); `summary_t25_2025[model][metric]` = `{orig, rewritten: {gap, lo, hi}, n_2025}` for the model selector's overview. The placebo is computed for the headline model only.
- **Lab-level counterpart** (`labs`, headline model): `labs.<t>.<scope>.<metric>.<version>` = `{gap, lo, hi, labs}`, the mean over labs of the within-lab equal-size gap (2023-2025, labs with at least 5 papers in each group, m <= 30), interval = bootstrap over labs; absent where fewer than 5 labs.
- **Sentences.** `sentences.text[metric]`: generated for the headline model at t = 25, 2025, all schools: "At >= 25% flagged, AI-written papers are higher/lower/not distinguishable from non-AI papers on <metric> in 2025 (gap [interval]; n); with wording held constant the gap is ...".
- No 2026 anywhere; `tests/test_ai_vs_not.py` checks group sizes, blanks, years and sentences.
- **Not matched on topic**: unlike `matched_test.json`, these groups are not matched on topic, school or year within a cell (only scope and year are fixed). AI-written papers sit disproportionately in ML and LLM topics, so a gap can come from topic mix; the `rewritten` series separates wording from the rest, not content from topic.
