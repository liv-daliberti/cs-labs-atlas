# SJTU recovered paper year review, 2026-11-15

The recovered publication proposals use OpenAlex work years. Fifty-seven of 396 work years differed from the year in the PI-owned publication listing. The union records the PI listing's year as `year`, preserves the OpenAlex year as `source_year`, and marks `year_basis: PI_publication_listing_review_20261115`. This is an edition choice, usually a preprint or online-first year versus the conference or journal issue year. The 20-paper hand check uses a new seed after this correction.

Examples checked against primary sources:

- *ImDy* has a 2024 arXiv record and an [ICLR 2025 paper](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=br8YB7KMug).
- *Detecting Backdoor Attacks Against Point Cloud Classifiers* has a 2021 arXiv record and a [2022 IEEE ICASSP presentation](https://rc.signalprocessingsociety.org/conferences/icassp-2022/spsicassp22vid0085).
- *Cross-Network Skip-Gram Embedding for Joint Network Alignment and Link Prediction* is listed in 2022 on the [PI's publication page](https://thinklab.sjtu.edu.cn/publications-rethinklab-20260823.html), while its DOI includes the online-first year 2020.
