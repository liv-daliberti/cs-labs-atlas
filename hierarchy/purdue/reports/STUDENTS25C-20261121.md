# Purdue third complete-roster student recovery, 2026-11-21

Purdue labs without students decrease from 289 to 251, a reduction of 38. The batch adds 456 student–lab records and preserves all 1,218 original records, identifiers, roles, phases and source claims. All 25 primary targets received a complete review of every named entry in their selected available current, alumni, graduate, undergraduate, visitor and CV supervision sections. Collection did not stop at one student. Verified individual co-adviser pairs also cover 13 additional previously empty labs; those additional labs are not represented as full-roster dossiers.

The official Purdue CS PhD-by-year page supplies individual adviser and completion-year records, including the Patrick T. Eugster and Wojciech Szpankowski entries. Undated alumni, unsupported initial identities, conflicting degree endpoints and unbound co-adviser phases remain explicit holds. No expected degree year was treated as a completed degree or future membership.
