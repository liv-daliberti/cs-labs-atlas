# Purdue second complete-roster student recovery, 2026-11-21

Purdue labs without students decrease from 324 to 289 (a reduction of 35). This batch adds 567 student–lab records while preserving all 651 existing records, their identifiers, roles, phases and source claims. All 25 primary targets received a complete review of every named entry in the selected available current, former, graduate, undergraduate, visiting, and CV supervision sections. Collection did not stop at one student. Individually source-supported co-advisers cover 10 additional previously empty labs; those additional labs are not represented as complete-roster reviews.

## Scope and enumeration

The outcome CSV retains all 2328 primary source observations: 960 accepted, 414 held, 952 excluded, 2 resolved. These are observations, including repeated names and separate phases, rather than distinct people. The co-adviser ledger retains 72 individually named claims, of which 13 are admitted. All 37 prior source groups are preserved. The research board retains 38 prior exact source reviews and adds 59, for 97 reusable reviews across both batches.

Every entry in the selected current/alumni rosters, graduate-advising tables and alumni biographies was checked. Explicitly dated research and degree phases were distinguished from employers, previous-school work, committee service and postdoctoral employment. Undated alumni were retained as unresolved observations. Expected degree years were not treated as completed degrees or future membership. Five historical NRL internship/scholar phases remain held because student enrollment during those visits is not individually proved; the valid current student phases remain. Conflicting degree endpoints, initials lacking positive identity bridges, and undefined co-adviser markers remain explicit.
