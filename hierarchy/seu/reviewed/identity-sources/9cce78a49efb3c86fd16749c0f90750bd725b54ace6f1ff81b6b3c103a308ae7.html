    Fan Liu (刘帆)

    About Me

    I am currently a Professor at Southeast University. I received my Ph.D degree from Shandong University, supervised by Prof. Liqiang Nie and Prof. Zhiyong Cheng. I served as a Research Fellow at the School of Computing, National University of Singapore, where I worked with Prof. Mohan Kankanhalli. I received the B.Eng. degree from Southeast University in China and the Master degree from Kansai University in Japan, under the supervision of Prof. Ebara Hiroyuki. My research interests include multimedia, information retrieval, and recommendation, with a focus on large language models and intelligent agents.
