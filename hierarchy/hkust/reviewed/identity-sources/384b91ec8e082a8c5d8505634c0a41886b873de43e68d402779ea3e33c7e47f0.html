Philosophy

I teach with the principle that there is no difference between dedication and talent over a sufficient time scale. Students who are interested in a subject and dedicated to succeed will excel at it. My main goal is to interest you in the topic: students who find the topic interesting will retain more, engage more, and continue to learn far more than I can teach within the 40-ish hours of lecture.

Need help?

If you need some more help in one of my courses, please feel free to contact me, either before/after class or by e-mail.

My general advice for struggling students is:

  • Finish assignments/exam studying ahead of time. There is no difference in the amount of work it takes to finish all assignments 3 days before the deadline or 3 minutes before the deadline - but you will be much more relaxed and flexible in the former case.
  • Engage with and question the material. I strongly encourage students to actively participate in class.
  • All of my courses use open-book exams, as I am not testing memorization. To excel at my exams, it is necessary to look beyond the bare facts conveyed in the text. Think on the level of design and principle.
