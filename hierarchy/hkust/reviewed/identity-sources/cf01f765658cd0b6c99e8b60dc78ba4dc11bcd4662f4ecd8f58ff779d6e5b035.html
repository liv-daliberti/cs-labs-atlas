Research Interests

Stochastic optimization and online optimization problems, with applications in supply chain management, revenue management, inventory management, and mechanism design.

Profile
Jiashuo JIANG joined the Department of Industrial Engineering and Decision Analytics at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology as an Assistant Professor in January 2023. He received Ph.D. in Operations Management from NYU Stern School of Business (2022), and B.S. degree in Mathematics from Peking University (2017). 
Honors and Awards
  • Finalist for the Jeff McGill Student Paper Award for Revenue Management and Pricing, 2021
  • Finalist for George E. Nicholson Student Paper Competition 2022
Selected Publications
  1. Jiashuo Jiang, Shixin Wang, Jiawei Zhang (2022), Achieving High Individual Service-Levels without Safety Stock? Optimal Rationing Policy of Pooled Resources, Operations Research.

  2. Shang Liu, Jiashuo Jiang, Xiaocheng Li (2022), Non-stationary Bandits with Knapsacks, Thirty-sixth Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS).

  3. Jiashuo Jiang, Will Ma, Jiawei Zhang (2022), Tight Guarantees for Multi-unit Prophet Inequalities and Online Stochastic Knapsack, ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms (SODA22).

  4. Jiashuo Jiang, Xiaocheng Li, Jiawei Zhang (2022), Online Stochastic Optimization with Wasserstein Based Non-stationarity, Under Review after a Major Revision at Management Science.

  5. Jiashuo Jiang, Will Ma, Jiawei Zhang (2022), Tightness without Counterexamples: A New Approach and New Results for Prophet Inequalities.

  6. Boxiao Chen, Jiashuo Jiang, Jiawei Zhang, Zhengyuan Zhou (2022), Learning to Order for Inventory Systems with Lost Sales and Uncertain Supplies Under a Major Revision at Management Science.

  7. Jiashuo Jiang, Will Ma, Jiawei Zhang (2022), Degeneracy is OK: Logarithmic Regret for Network Revenue Management with Indiscrete Distributions, Submitted.
