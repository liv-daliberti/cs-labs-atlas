Research Interest form

Yudai Tanaka is an incoming Assistant Professor of Computer Science at UT Austin and is currently a PhD candidate at University of Chicago advised by Pedro Lopes. His work is supported by Google PhD Fellowship and Siebel Scholars Program.

His research explores computer interfaces that generate sensory feedback by interfacing directly with users’ brains and nervous systems (e.g., transmitting signals straight to nerves; or even delivering senses directly to the brain). Yudai envisions that these interface as the foundation for the next generation of interactive systems, able to augment physical abilities and accelerate skill acquisition.

Yudai has published his work in top Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) venues, including ACM CHI & UIST, earning 🏆 Best Paper Award (CHI’23), 🏅 Best Paper Honorable Mentions (CHI’24, UIST’24) and ⚙ Best Demo Awards (CHI’22, UIST’24). His research has been featured in IEEE Spectrum, New Scientist, and CBS.
