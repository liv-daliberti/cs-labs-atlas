website (people/members page and publications page), record every member who was at the school in 2016-2026 with
exact-quote evidence, and point the build at the lab's publications page(s) so the publications missing from our data
get listed. Your only output file is ONE Python decision module:
    T/dec_<BATCH>.py            e.g. dec_princeton_03.py, dec_mit_11.py
Don't write or edit anything else under hierarchy/ (not the tools, dossiers, other dec_ modules, school stores, or
evidence files). The coordinator merges stores and builds the outputs.

  Keep Wayback use to people/members pages (wb3 default --n 3); don't fetch Wayback for publications pages.

PER LAB
 1. Site. Start from the dossier (lab_url, the EECS/CSAIL/CS profile 'Website' links, the PI homepage). Find the
    people/members/team/students page (may be a section of the PI's homepage or a group page) and the publications
    page. JS-loaded directories: look in the raw HTML (f.py --head with a large N, or --grep "\.json|\.ya?ml") for the data
    file and fetch it (robots honoured); quotes then come from its raw text.
 2. Members: everyone listed who was at the school in 2016-2026 - PhD students, postdocs, masters (SM, MEng, MSE, MS),
    undergraduates (UROP, SuperUROP, senior thesis, REU), research staff (scientists, engineers, programmers: role
    'other' + note), visitors (role 'other' + note), plain 'members' with no role ('member'). Use the live page plus up
    to 3 Wayback captures spread over the years (wb3.py) to catch alumni.
    Leave out: the PI; non-humans (lab pets); faculty/collaborators/co-PIs listed only as such; alumni whose stated
    dates end before 2016; people at the PI's previous institution (a page from before the PI arrived, or a section
    labelled with the other school) unless the source places them at this school. Undated alumni: include only when
    some source shows them at the school in or after 2016 (e.g. listed as current on a 2016+ capture); otherwise leave
    them out and say so in notes.
    name = as written (keep nicknames like "Liv", initials, accents). role in phd|postdoc|masters|undergraduate|member|
    other. years = [first, last] only when the source states them ([None, 2021] for "PhD 2021"; an undergrad cohort
    heading "2025" -> [2025, 2025]); else None.
    One row per person per source is fine: build merges the same person seen on several captures into one row with
    extra sources; put the live page first. Matching against students_held (nicknames from nicknames/names.csv +
    extras.csv, initials, "Last, First", accents, hyphens, middle names, swapped order) is automatic in build: check
    the -v output. If a NEW row is in fact a held person under a variant build cannot see (a nickname missing from the
    tables, a changed surname), say so in notes ("X = held Y"); don't drop the row.
 3. Publications: if the lab or the PI has a publications page (HTML list, JSON data file, or .bib), add
    Pubs(url, spec). Run pubx.py first, check a sample with --all, and tune spec until titles and years are right
    (no author lists, venues or 'PDF' as titles; years not taken from page dates). Include both the lab page and the
    PI's own page when both exist (build dedups by title). Not: coauthors' or department pages, Google Scholar, dblp,
    PDF CVs. If no spec works and the page has <= 30 entries in 2016-2026, list them with P(...); otherwise describe
    it in notes. Don't judge whether a missing paper counts for the lab (another tool checks affiliation).
 4. D[pi] = Lab(...) for EVERY PI in the batch, even when nothing is found (Lab(notes="no lab site found: checked ...")).
    notes: pages used (with Wayback capture years), what was left out and why, held people seen under a variant,
    blocked sites ("blocked: <host> bot challenge / 403 / robots"), anything odd.
