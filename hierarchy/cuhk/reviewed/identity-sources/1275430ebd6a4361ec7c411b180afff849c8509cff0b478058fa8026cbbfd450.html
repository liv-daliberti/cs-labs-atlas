Research Interests

I work on systems and networking. I lead the NetX Lab. Welcome to visit us!

Our research is/was supported by funding from

  • Amazon Web Services, Azure, ByteDance, Huawei, Microsoft Research Asia, etc.

Current Projects

  • AI systems: AI is important, but infra and deployment support is often the bottleneck to real-world use

    • Agentic systems, support the compound workflows of agents with complex reasoning paths, inter-model communication, and external tool calling

    • LLM performance, faster, cheaper, without being dumber that is…

    • Reliability, fault-tolerant systems that won't break, and AIOps that autonomously troubleshoot incidents, for hyperscale infra

    • Simulation, key to understand the trade-offs and scaling laws of training and inference

    • Related pubs: Frontier, Echo@SC’26, Silverstone@SIGCOMM’26, FOCUS@ICML’26, PRISM@MLSys’26, TSGuard@FSE’26, DSV@ASPLOS’26, Mycroft@SOSP’25, StitchLLM@ACL’25, Ayo@ASPLOS’25, EMNLP’23, Lina@ATC’23, Lyra@EuroSys’23

  • Networking: building the future network

    • Programmable dataplane, how can we better use the SmartNICs/DPUs?

    • ML-empowered network management, how can ML help reduce the human effort needed in network management tasks?

    • Related pubs: RoutingBench@NeurIPS’26, SmartSwitch@NSDI’26, Matt@APNet’26, Xumi, Yala@ASPLOS’25, Meili, NAssim@SIGCOMM’22, DQN@SIGCOMM’22, INFOCOM’24, CoNEXT’22, ATC’20, MM’23, INFOCOM’23

  • Learning: we also study theory and algorithm on the side

    • Learning to Optimize (L2O), how can we leverage DL to help solve optimization programs?

    • Related pubs: AGZO@ICML’26, Learning for GD@NeulIPS’25, Step-sizes in Quasi-Newton, CVPR’24, INFOCOM’24
