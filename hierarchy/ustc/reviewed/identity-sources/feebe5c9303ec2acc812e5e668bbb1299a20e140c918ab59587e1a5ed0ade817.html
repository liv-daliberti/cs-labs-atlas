    Profile

    Zhihui Li, Tenure-Track Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor, is a nationally recognized youth talent. Her research focuses on artificial intelligence and computer vision, encompassing embodied intelligence, multimodal large models, and video understanding. She has published over 50 academic papers in top international journals and conferences, such as IEEE TPAMI, CVPR, and ICLR, as the first or corresponding author. She serves on the editorial boards of several prestigious international journals, including ACM TOMM, and has acted as an Area Chair or Senior Program Committee member more than 10 times for CCF-A conferences. She has led multiple research projects, including one funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China Youth Project.
