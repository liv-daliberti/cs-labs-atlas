About Me

Hey there! I’m a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Virginia Tech, where I work with Eric Pauley. My research spans network security and internet measurement, with a focus on identifying and mitigating emerging threats. Beyond my core research, I’m particularly interested in the role of artificial intelligence and how its gonna shape the future of computer security and how it can be applied to make the internet more secure.

When I’m not researching, I’m usually traveling for nature or concerts, or burning off steam at the gym.
