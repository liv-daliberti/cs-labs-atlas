                Brian Keith

                Contact:

                briankeithn@vt.edu
                • Website

                Brian Keith was a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science. He was co-advised by Chris North and Tanushree Mitra. 

                Keith's research focus was on online information narratives, in particular, how to represent, extract, and visualize them. He is also interested in analyzing how misinformation spreads in these narratives.
