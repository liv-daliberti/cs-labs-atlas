Biography: Peng Gao is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Virginia Tech and a Faculty Fellow of the Commonwealth Cyber Initiative (CCI). He is a Senior Member of both IEEE and ACM. Previously, he was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of California, Berkeley (with Dawn Song). He received his Ph.D. from Princeton University (with Prateek Mittal, Sanjeev R. Kulkarni) and his B.E. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University. His research lies at the intersection of security and privacy, systems and networking, and AI, with a recent focus on agentic cyber defense, secure agentic AI, agentic formal verification, and trustworthy computing and networked systems. His work has frequently appeared in leading venues including IEEE S&P, USENIX Security, CCS, NDSS, Euro S&P, ATC, VLDB, ICDE, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, KDD, ICSE, and IEEE TDSC, resulting in multiple patents and wide industry adoption. He regularly serves on the program committees of major security and systems conferences and is currently an Associate Editor of IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing (TDSC) and IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security (TIFS). His honors include the NSF CAREER Award, CSAW Applied Research Finalist (2018), Microsoft Security AI Research Award (2020), Amazon Research Award (2021, 2026), Cisco Research Award (2021), Meta Research Award Finalist (2021), Amazon–VT Initiative Faculty Research Award (2022), and Google Academic Research Award (2025).

Research Interests

Agentic AI for Security, AI Safety and Security, Systems and Network Security, Human-Centered AI and Security, AI for Software Engineering and Science
