Bio

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at KAIST. I received my PhD from Virginia Tech in 2025, advised by Tijay Chung. Before joining VT, I finished my bachelor from Zhejiang University CS Department in 2020.

My research focuses on measuring, understanding, and securing Internet infrastructure. I conduct Internet-scale measurements to uncover real-world behaviors, risks, and dependencies, and design systems and protocols to improve the security, performance, and resilience of the Internet.
