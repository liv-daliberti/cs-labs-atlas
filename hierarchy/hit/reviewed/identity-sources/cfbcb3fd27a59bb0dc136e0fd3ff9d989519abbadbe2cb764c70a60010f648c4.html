  • Wu Rui
      title

      Rui Wu, Ph.D., Associate Professor, Doctoral Supervisor, Deputy Director of the Computer Systems Teaching and Research Section, IEEE Member, CCF Member, Committee Member of the Education Committee and the Intelligent Human-Computer Interaction Committee of the Heilongjiang Provincial Computer Society.

      Research interests: pattern recognition, machine learning, 3D simulation and modeling, multimodal data analysis, chaotic image encryption, etc. Principal investigator of projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, sub-projects of national key R&D programs, the Natural Science Foundation of Heilongjiang Province, the HIT Innovation Fund, and the PowerChina Group. Has published more than 80 papers indexed by SCI and EI in high-level domestic and international journals and conferences. In the past three years, over 10 papers have been published as first or corresponding author in journals ranked Q1 by the Chinese Academy of Sciences or JCR, including five ESI highly cited papers and one hot paper. Holds four authorized invention patents as the first inventor.


      Admission Information

      1. Recruiting 1–2 Ph.D. students for the 2026 cohort. Research topics: multimodal learning and (multi-agent) reinforcement learning.

      2. Recruiting master’s students and outstanding undergraduates for the 2026 cohort. Machine-vision topics: small-object detection, robotic perception, 3D motion generation. Machine-learning topics: multimodal learning and reinforcement learning.
