  • Profile
      title

      Zhao Lingling is an associate professor and doctoral supervisor in Faculty of Computing, Harbin Institute of Technology. She conducted research in multimodal machine learning methods and applications. She has undertaken projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and national key research & development program during the 14th Five-Year Plan period etc. She has also participated in more than ten national-level scientific research projects. She has published over 60 papers in top-tier computer-related journals and conferences, and has received awards such as the Special Prize for Scientific and Technological Progress from the Beijing Society of Surveying and Mapping in 2025.
