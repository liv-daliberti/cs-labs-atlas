      名称

      张伟哲教授是哈尔滨工业大学教授、博导，入选国家级人才计划。主要研究方向是网络空间安全、高性能计算、嵌入式计算和云计算。发表期刊和会议论文约 290 篇，著作 3 本。担任IEEE Transactions on Cloud Computing的编委。获得黑龙江省科学技术奖科技进步2项。现任国家自然科学基金信息学部评审会专家、国家重点研发计划项目评审会专家、中文信息学会评测工委会主任，中国计算机学会嵌入式系统专委常委，中国计算机学会高性能计算专委、系统软件专委委员，中国互联网协会推广与普及工作委员会委员，ACM 高级会员, IEEE 高级会员, CCF杰出会员。

  • Basic Information
      名称

      Weizhe Zhang is a Professor and Doctoral Supervisor at Harbin Institute of Technology. His main research areas include cyberspace security, high-performance computing, embedded computing, and cloud computing. He has published approximately 290 journal and conference papers and authored three books. He serves as an editorial board member for IEEE Transactions on Cloud Computing.

      Professor Zhang has received two Heilongjiang Provincial Science and Technology Progress Awards. He is an expert reviewer for the Information Science Department of the National Natural Science Foundation of China and for the National Key R&D Program of China. He also holds several key positions, including Director of the Evaluation Working Committee of the Chinese Information Processing Society, Standing Committee Member of the CCF Technical Committee on Embedded Systems, and Member of the CCF Technical Committees on High Performance Computing and System Software. Additionally, he is a Member of the Promotion and Popularization Working Committee of the Internet Society of China, as well as a Senior Member of ACM, a Senior Member of IEEE, and a Distinguished Member of CCF.
