      名称

      宋彬彬，博士，副教授，神舟青年学者，国家级青年人才。2018年于电子科技大学获得本科学位，2024年于澳门大学取得博士学位。2024年至2025年于新加坡南洋理工大学担任博士后研究员。现就职于哈尔滨工业大学计算学部智能接口与人机交互研究中心。主要研究方向为图像与视频的复原、医学图像增强和三维场景重建，在IJCV、TCSVT、ICCV、ECCV、AAAI等期刊会议发表十余篇学术论文，并担任TMM、TCSVT、ACMMM、ICCV、AAAI、ICME等期刊会议审稿人。

  • Biography
      title

      Dr. Binbin Song is an associate professor with the Department of Computer Science, Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT), Harbin, China. He received the B.S. degree in measurement and control technology and instruments from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, in 2018 and the Ph.D. degree from the Department of Computer and Information Science, University of Macau, in 2024. From October 2024 to June 2025, he worked as a post-doctoral research fellow at the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering in Nanyang Technological University, working with Prof. Bihan Wen. His research interests include low-level computer vision, medical image enhancement, and 3D vision.
