      title

      Doctor, Master’s Supervisor, Associate Professor

      Mobile Number: 15245084413

      Email: hzguo@hit.edu.cn

      Mailing Address: Harbin Institute of Technology, Science and Technology Innovation Building J2007


      Hongzhe Guo, Associate Professor and Master's Supervisor at the School of Computer Science, Harbin Institute of Technology, core member of the national key research and development project "China's 100,000 Genomes Project", and member of the 'Leading Goose' program in Heilongjiang Province. Main research areas: genomic sequencing data analysis, single-cell sequencing data analysis, DNA storage, and biobig data security. He has published multiple academic papers in top bioinformatics journals such as Journal of Infection (IF=38.637), Bioinformatics (IF=6.937), GPB (IF=9.5), CIBM (IF=7.7), TCBB (IF=4.5), and BMC Bioinformatics (IF=3.307). He has presided over one Youth Project of the National Natural Science Foundation, one General Project of the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation, and a sub-project of the National Key Research and Development Program. Currently, he is a reviewer for journals such as Genome Biology, BMC Bioinformatics, IEEE-ACM Transactions on Computational Biology and Bioinformatics, and Frontiers in Genetics, as well as a member of the Computational Biology and Bioinformatics Committee of the Chinese Society of Biomedical Engineering and the Data Science and Big Data Technology Committee of the Heilongjiang Computer Society.


      Each year, he recruits 2-3 master's students.Students are welcome to join us in decoding the genetic information of life using computers!
