  • Basic Information
      title

      Liu Yongzhuang, Professor at Harbin Institute of Technology, Ph.D. supervisor, visiting scholar at Duke University, USA, Vice Secretary-General of the Internet Health Management Branch of the China Health Management Association, member of the Rare Tumor Professional Committee of the China Rare Disease Alliance, and reviewer for renowned bioinformatics international journals such as Briefings in Bioinformatics and Bioinformatics. He is responsible for one major project under the "Science and Technology Innovation 2030 – Cancer, Cardiovascular, Respiratory, and Metabolic Disease Prevention and Treatment Research" program, two projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, one special funding project from the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation, and one project under the "Revealing and Tackling Key Technological Challenges" initiative of the Heilongjiang Provincial Department of Science and Technology. As a key member, he has participated in multiple national key R&D plans and major projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China. Liu has published over 30 academic papers as the first author or corresponding author in top bioinformatics journals such as Briefings in Bioinformatics, Bioinformatics, and Genome Biology. His research on "Genomic Big Data Analysis Theories and Methods" has won the first prize in the provincial-level Natural Science Awards.

  • Research Interests
      title

      • Research on Large Multi-Modal Models, AI Agents, reinforcement learning, etc., exploring innovations in data representation and model architecture.

      • Research on new artificial intelligence algorithms, model, and frameworks, integrating and analyzing multi-modal and multi-omics data such as genomic data, proteomic data, clinical imaging data, and electronic health records, aiming at precise diagnosis of diseases and innovative drug development.
