  • Basic Info
      title

      Han Xixian, Ph.D., Professor, and Doctoral Supervisor, Distinguished Member of CCF (China Computer Federation), Executive Committee Member of the Chinese Database Technical Committee, and selected into the Provincial High-Level Talent Program. His research focuses on big data analysis and mining. He has published more than 50 papers in international conferences, journals, and leading Chinese journals, including ICDE, CIKM, DASFAA, ICDCS, TKDE, IOTJ, INS, KAIS, FGCS, WWW, ESWA, IPL, TST, Journal of Computer Science and Technology, and Journal of Computer Research and Development. Among these, over 30 papers appeared in CCF Class A/B conferences and journals or JCR Q1/Q2 journals, including 7 papers in CCF Tier-1 journals, with several papers cited more than 100 times. He currently serves as a reviewer for international journals such as ACM TODS, IEEE TKDE, IEEE TNNLS, and IEEE TII. In recent years, he has led more than ten research projects, including key projects of the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC), NSFC General and Young Investigator Projects, the Natural Science Foundation of Shandong Province, and various industry-funded projects. He received the Second Prize of the Weihai Science and Technology Award in 2016 and holds more than ten granted invention patents.
