  • 个人简介
      名称

      梁志宇，男，汉族，计算学部海量数据计算研究中心，副教授，博士生导师。研究方向为时序大数据管理与分析、时序隐私计算、工业大数据、AI for Engineering等。担任CCF数据库专委会、系统软件专委会执行委员。主持国家自然科学基金、智能电网国家科技重大专项子课题、中国博士后面上项目，作为骨干参与国家自然科学基金、国家重点研发计划等多个项目。在The VLDB Journal、TKDE、SIGMOD、VLDB、ICDE、KDD、ICML等顶级期刊会议发表学术论文20余篇。担任TKDE、ICML、CIKM等多个国际期刊和会议的审稿人。获得吴文俊人工智能技术发明二等奖、中国计算机学会计算机应用创新技术一等奖、国家资助博士后研究人员计划、国家博士后科研业绩评估考核资助、黑龙江省计算机学会优博、ACM中国(哈尔滨)优博、哈尔滨工业大学校优博等荣誉和奖励。

  • About me
      title

      Zhiyu Liang is now an Associate Professor with Harbin Institute of Technology. He has been PI for National Natural Science Foundation of China, sub-project of the National Science and Technology Major Project, and China Postdoctoral Science Foundation. He has also participated in multiple national projects. His Ph.D. thesis was selected to be outstanding Ph.D. dissertation of Heilongjiang Computer Federation and Harbin Institute of Technology. His research work has been published at top venues, such as PVLDB, SIGMOD, ICDE, KDD, ICML, VLDBJ and TKDE, etc. He serves as a reviewer/PC member for international journals and conferences, such as IEEE TKDE, DMKD, KAIS, ICML, CIKM, etc.


      His research interests mainly include:

      ·AI-powered time series data management and analysis

      ·Federated data computing and learning

      ·Data science for IoT scenarios such as smart grid, manufacturing and transportation.


      Seeking highly self-motivated Ph.D / master students and undergraduate interns. Feel free to contact him if you are interested in the related topics.
