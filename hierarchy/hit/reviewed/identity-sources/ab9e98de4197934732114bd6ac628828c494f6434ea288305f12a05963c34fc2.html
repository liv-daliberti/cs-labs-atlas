  • 研究领域
      名称

      1. 光电信息技术与仪器工程

      主要研究方向:

      （1）大尺度多自由度坐标测量理论与技术

      以解决大型装备制造业的现场零部件及装配过程中的三维几何测试和工装夹具在线校准等技术关键和难题为目标，研究大尺度坐标测量的新理论、新技术和新设备。主要研究包括基于串/并联结构的多自由度坐标测量理论与技术；单目/多目视觉坐标测量理论和校准技术。

      （2）运动目标三维视觉探测、识别与跟踪技术

      主要研究：目标的运动参数测量技术；空间目标跟踪和识别技术；交会对接技术，视觉相对导航技术，高速嵌入式图像采集系统及图像处理技术。

    • 基本信息
        title

        Dong Ye, Doctor of Engineering, professor, doctoral supervisor. Vice Dean, School of Instrument Science and Engineering, Harbin Institute of Technology.

        My main research direction is the theory and calibration techniques of visual coordinate measurement; Three dimensional visual detection, recognition, and tracking technology for moving targets; Multi sensor environmental perception and autonomous navigation technology. Hosted and completed more than 40 research projects, including the National Natural Science Foundation, National Defense and Military Technology Foundation, and Aerospace Academy. Won 1 first prize and 1 second prize for ministerial level technological inventions, 2 second prizes for provincial-level teaching achievements, and published over 80 SCI and EI indexed papers. I am currently the Vice Chairman of the 8th Committee of the Precision Engineering Society of the China Instrument and Control Society, and a member of the Professional Committee of Metrology and Testing of China Aerospace Society.
