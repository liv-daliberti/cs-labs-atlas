      • 招生方向

      1. 非连续控制理论及在智能机器人/机械臂中应用

      滑模控制理论及抗干扰控制在智能机器人/机械臂中的应用，主要包括机械臂最优运动轨迹规划、高精度轨迹跟踪，优化及控制以及多机械臂的协同控制问题。


      2. 新型电力系统/智能微电网

      主要包括新能源电力系统/智能微电网的智能控制及优化运行等。此外，研究包括多种电力变换器的调制和控制方法，如二电平、三电平、多电平模块变换器。


      3. 大语言模型及在工业系统中的应用（如高铁、大宗商品等）

      主要包括工业系统数据理解与知识建模、工业运维与智能决策、工业人机交互与智能助手、工业人机交互与智能助手等方面的研究。


      4. 精密运动控制方向

      主要包括永磁同步电机无位置传感器控制技术、高速高精度运动跟踪控制算法。

  • Basic Information
      title

      He is a Professor and Doctoral Supervisor at the School of Astronautics, Harbin Institute of Technology, and a recipient of the National Natural Science Foundation of China Excellent Young Scholars Fund. His research primarily focuses on discontinuous control theory and applications, intelligent robot/robotic arm control, large language models and intelligent tagging, and intelligent microgrids. He has led more than 10 national-level projects and over 20 provincial- and ministerial-level projects. His research achievements have received two First Prizes of the Heilongjiang Provincial Natural Science Award (ranked first and fourth, respectively). He was selected as a member of the third cohort of National Huang Danian-Style Teacher Teams in Chinese universities and was honored as a National Outstanding Postdoctoral Fellow (2020). He received the China’s 100 Most Influential International Academic Papers Award (first author/ranked first) and the IFAC Best Conference Paper Award (ranked first). He has been consecutively recognized as a Clarivate Highly Cited Researcher and consecutively included in Stanford University’s World’s Top 2% Scientists list, both the Career-Long Impact and Single-Year Impact lists. He has published more than 100 SCI-indexed journal papers, with over 10,000 SCI citations and an h-index of 59, and has been granted more than 30 national invention patents. He currently serves as an editorial board member or associate editor for more than ten authoritative SCI journals at home and abroad, including IEEE Transactions on Industrial Electronics (IEEE-TIE) and IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering (IEEE-TASE), and has also served as a special session chair for multiple international conferences.

      ☆ Research Directions for Graduate Admissions：

      1. Discontinuous Control Theory and Applications in Intelligent Robots/Robotic Arms

      This research direction focuses on the application of sliding mode control theory and disturbance-rejection control to intelligent robots and robotic arm, mainly including optimal motion trajectory planning for robotic arms, high-precision trajectory tracking, optimization and control, as well as cooperative control of multiple robotic arms.

      2. New Eelectricity Systems / Intelligent Microgrids

      This direction mainly covers intelligent control and optimal operation of new energy power systems and intelligent microgrids. It also includes research on modulation and control methods for various power converters, such as two-level, three-level, and modular multilevel converters.

      3. Large Language Models and Applications in Industrial Systems (e.g., High-Speed Railways, Bulk Commodities, etc.)

      This research direction focuses on data understanding and knowledge modeling for industrial systems, intelligent operation and maintenance, intelligent decision-making, and industrial human–machine interaction and assistant systems.

      4. Precision Motion Control

      This direction mainly includes sensorless control of permanent magnet synchronous motors and high-speed, high-precision motion tracking control algorithms.
