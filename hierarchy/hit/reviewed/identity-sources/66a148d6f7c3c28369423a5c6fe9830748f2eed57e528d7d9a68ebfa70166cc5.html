  • Basic Information
      title

      Ji Zhenzhou, Ph.D., is a professor and doctoral supervisor at Harbin Institute of Technology, Weihai. He serves as Director of the Parallel and Intelligent Computing Research Center, a member of a ministry-level science and technology expert group, and an expert reviewer for national science and technology projects and national-level scientific and technological achievements. He previously served as Deputy Director of the CCF Technical Committee on Computer Architecture and Deputy Dean of the School of Computer Science and Technology. His research focuses primarily on intelligent computing and high-performance computing. He is committed to overcoming the computing-power and storage bottlenecks faced by large models from the perspectives of both model architecture and hardware platforms, with the goal of building an independently controllable, high-efficiency, high-performance large-scale computing support platform.

  • Major Achievements
      title

      He has long been engaged in research on high-performance computing, artificial intelligence, high-performance computing-power support, intelligent computing, computer architecture, and security. He has led and completed three projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, three ministry-level science and technology projects, and three Aerospace 863 Program projects. He has received the Third Prize for Scientific and Technological Progress from the Commission of Science, Technology and Industry for National Defense, the Third Prize for Scientific and Technological Progress from China Aerospace Corporation, and the Third Prize for Scientific and Technological Progress of Heilongjiang Province. He has published more than 100 high-level SCI- and EI-indexed papers and has been granted more than 10 patents.
