      名称

      1. 高端装备精密运动控制：包括高速度高精度跟踪控制方法、运动规划与控制一体化研究。

      3. 无人驾驶&智能汽车：侧重于无人驾驶下的路面识别、摄像头&激光雷达等多源传感信息融合技术；智能汽车故障及传播机理分析、整车一体化容错控制等。

  • Brief Introduction
      名称

      Sun Weichao received his bachelor's degree from Central South University in Automation, and his master's and doctoral degrees from Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT) in Control Science and Engineering. He is currently a tenured professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Astronautics, HIT. He is a recipient of the National Excellent Young Scientist Fund and serves as the director of the HIT Young Scientist Studio.


      He has long been engaged in research on control theory and applications, achieving significant results in fields such as precision motion control, intelligent robotics, and automotive control. He has published over 100 SCI-indexed journal papers, which have received more than 5,000 SCI citations, earning him recognition as a Highly Cited Researcher. He has also authored two academic monographs and holds 22 authorized national invention patents. He has led more than ten major national and provincial-level projects, including key projects and the Excellent Young Scientist Fund from the National Natural Science Foundation of China, General Program projects, and the Ningbo Major Scientific and Technological "Open Bidding for Selecting the Best Candidates" Project. His accolades include the First Prize for Technological Progress from the Chinese Association of Automation (ranked first among second-place recipients), the Second Prize for Natural Science from Heilongjiang Province (ranked first), the Best Paper Award at the IEEE International Conference on Information and Automation, and the Outstanding Doctoral Dissertation Award from HIT. He serves as an associate editor for international journals such as IEEE Transactions on Mechatronics and IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics: Systems. Additionally, he has served as a Track Chair or Special Session Chair at nine international conferences, including IFAC and IECON.
