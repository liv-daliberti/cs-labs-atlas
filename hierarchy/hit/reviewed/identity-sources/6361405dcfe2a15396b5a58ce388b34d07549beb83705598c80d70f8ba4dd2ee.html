  • Basic Information
      title

      Xuejian Cui, an associate professor of the Faculty of Computing, doctoral supervisor, and Shenzhou Young Scholar.

      I obtained my bachelor's degree from Beihang University in 2019 and my PhD degree from Tsinghua University in 2025. Currently, I am employed at the Bioinformatics Research Center of the Faculty of Computing at Harbin Institute of Technology.

      My research focuses on intelligent analysis algorithms for biological big data and biomedical large language models. I have published multiple academic papers in journals such as Nature Methods (2025), Nature Computational Science (2024), Nature Communications (2024, 2025a, 2025b), and Nucleic Acids Research (2021a, 2021b, 2026).

      I served as a reviewer for journals such as Nature Machine Intelligence and Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence.
