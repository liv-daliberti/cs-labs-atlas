  • Basic Information
      title

      Dr. Li Chunshan, Associate Professor and Doctoral Supervisor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Harbin Institute of Technology. He has successively presided over more than 20 provincial and ministerial level projects, including the National Natural Science Foundation, National Key R&D Projects, Shandong Provincial Key R&D Projects, and Shandong Provincial Natural Science Foundation Projects. Received 1 first prize in Artificial Intelligence Science Progress from the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence, 1 first prize and 1 third prize in Shandong Province Science and Technology Progress, 1 second prize in Technology Progress from the China Electric Power Promotion Association, and 1 first prize in Power Innovation from the Shandong Electric Power Industry Association. Published over 40 academic papers in CCF A and B categories, including IEEE TCSVT, TGRS, Information Fusion, NeurIPS, ICML, ACMMM, VLDB, and COLING.
