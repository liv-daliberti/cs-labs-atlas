  • 基本信息
      名称

      万照麟，博士，计算学部副研究员，博士生导师。中国图象图形学学会会员机器视觉专委会、情感计算与理解专委会、女科技工作者工委会委员，中国计算机学会多媒体专委会执行委员。2020年于哈尔滨工业大学取得博士学位。2018年至2019年在美国德克萨斯大学奥斯汀分校做访问学者。现就职于哈尔滨工业大学计算学部智能接口与人机交互研究中心。


      主要研究方向包括多媒体信息处理、图像视频质量评价、三维视觉、虚拟现实、AI4S、大模型Agent及人工智能赋能教育等。主持国家自然科学基金青年项目、重点研发计划子课题、国家资助博士后研究人员计划B档（博新计划上会）、中国博士后科学基金面上项目、黑龙江省自然科学基金联合引导项目、黑龙江省博士后面上资助、中央高校基本科研业务基金等科研项目；主持校级数智化实践教学创新项目及数字化导学模式研究等教学改革项目；参与多项国家973 计划、国家重点研发计划、国家自然科学基金面上项目等。在科研论文方面，目前已发表学术论文30余篇，其中包括TCSVT、TMM、CVPR、AAAI、MM、DCC等国际权威期刊会议。

  • Basic Information
      title

      Zhaolin Wan, Ph.D., Associate Research Fellow of the School of Computer Science, and Ph.D. supervisor. Member of the China Society of Image and Graphics, serving on the Technical Committee of Machine Vision, the Technical Committee of Affective Computing and Understanding, the Committee of Women Scientists, and the Executive Committee Member of the Technical Committee on Multimedia, China Computer Federation. She received her Ph.D. degree from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2020. From 2018 to 2019, she was a visiting scholar at the University of Texas at Austin, USA. She is currently with the Intelligent Interface and Human-Computer Interaction Research Center, School of Computer Science, Harbin Institute of Technology.


      Her main research interests include multimedia information processing, image and video quality assessment, 3D vision, and virtual reality. She has presided over one National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) Youth Program, one sub-project of the National Key R&D Program, one National Postdoctoral Program for Innovative Talents (Category B), one China Postdoctoral Science Foundation General Project, one Heilongjiang Provincial Natural Science Foundation Joint Guidance Project, one Heilongjiang Provincial Postdoctoral General Project, and two Fundamental Research Funds for the Central Universities, among others. She has also participated in several major national projects, including the National Basic Research Program (973 Program), the National Key R&D Program, and the NSFC General Program. To date, she has published more than 20 academic papers, including in prestigious international journals and conferences such as TCSVT, TMM, CVPR, MM, and DCC.
