      Dr. Xianming Liu is a Professor, Ph.D advisor with the Department of Computer Science, Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT), Harbin, China. He received the B.S., M.S., and Ph.D degrees in computer science from HIT under the supervision of Prof. Debin Zhao and Prof. Wen Gao, in 2006, 2008 and 2012, respectively. In 2007, he joined the Joint Research and Development Lab (JDL), Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing, as a research assistant. From 2009 to 2012, he was with National Engineering Lab for Video Technology, Peking University, Beijing, as a research assistant. In 2011, he spent half a year at the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, McMaster University, Canada, as a visiting student. From December 2012 to December 2013, he worked as a post-doctoral fellow at McMaster University, Hamilton, Canada, working with Prof. Xiaolin Wu. From January 2014 to March 2017, he worked with Prof. Gene Cheung as a project researcher at National Institute of Informatics (NII), Tokyo, Japan. He is the receipt of IEEE ICME 2016 Best Student Paper Award and IFTC2016 Best Paper Award. His research interests include trustworthy AI, 3D signal processing and biomedical signal processing.

      【研究方向】

      1. 可信赖人工智能

      面向开放复杂环境，发展新一代具备可解释性、可泛化性、可对齐性的人工智能基础理论与关键技术。重点研究人工智能的可信性验证、透明决策机制、安全与隐私保护方法，以及符合人类伦理规范与社会价值的智能系统构建。


      2. 资源受限条件下的人工智能

      针对边缘计算与端侧部署场景中的计算、存储与通信受限问题，研究高效能人工智能模型与系统。包括：大模型压缩与知识蒸馏、推理加速与能效优化、自适应轻量化模型架构设计、AI Infra与分布式计算框架，推动人工智能在资源受限环境中的普适应用。


      3. 具身智能

      发展面向复杂动态环境的世界模型与具身智能体。研究自动驾驶、无人系统中的先进感知与认知方法，探索物理世界到数字孪生空间的映射与交互机制，实现感知—决策—执行的闭环优化，推动智能体在真实场景下的稳健性与自适应能力。


      4. AI驱动的图像与视频编码

      探索人工智能在多媒体数据处理与压缩编码中的新范式。发展基于深度学习与生成模型的高效图像/视频编码算法，研究感知优化、内容自适应压缩与跨模态编码机制，以应对超高清视频、沉浸式交互和智能通信场景下的海量数据传输与存储挑战。

      2. 科研素养：具备严谨的学术态度、独立思考能力与团队合作精神，热衷于探索人工智能及相关领域的前沿问题。

      3. 发展志向：立志投身学术前沿研究，关注人工智能在国家重大需求与战略任务中的应用与落地。

      4. 综合素质：勇于迎接挑战，具有开拓创新精神和持久科研热情，能够在跨学科背景下开展系统性研究。

  • Research Interests
      名称


      Broadly interested in multimedia signal processing, biomedical signal processing and machine learning:

      1. Trustworthy AI, including robust learning with imperfect labels, few/zero-shot learning, federated learning, adversarial attack and defense, etc;

      2. Computational imaging, including low-level image reconstruction and enhancement, microscopy imaging, etc;

      3. Biomedical signal compression, including lossless/near-lossless compression for medical images and genomics data;

      4. 3D signal processing and analysis, including mesh denoising, point clouds usampling, point clouds analysis.

  • Biography
      title

      Dr. Xianming Liu is a Professor, Ph.D advisor with the Department of Computer Science, Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT), Harbin, China. He received the B.S., M.S., and Ph.D degrees in computer science from HIT under the supervision of Prof. Debin Zhao and Prof. Wen Gao, in 2006, 2008 and 2012, respectively. In 2007, he joined the Joint Research and Development Lab (JDL), Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing, as a research assistant. From 2009 to 2012, he was with National Engineering Lab for Video Technology, Peking University, Beijing, as a research assistant. In 2011, he spent half a year at the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, McMaster University, Canada, as a visiting student. From December 2012 to December 2013, he worked as a post-doctoral fellow at McMaster University, Hamilton, Canada, working with Prof. Xiaolin Wu. From January 2014 to March 2017, he worked with Prof. Gene Cheung as a project researcher at National Institute of Informatics (NII), Tokyo, Japan. He is the receipt of IEEE ICME 2016 Best Student Paper Award and IFTC2016 Best Paper Award. His research interests include trustworthy AI, 3D signal processing and biomedical signal processing.
