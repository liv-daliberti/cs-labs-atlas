  • Basic Information
      title

      Dr. Kai Wang is an Associate Professor and Doctoral Supervisor at Faculty of Computing, Harbin Institute of Technology. Before that, he was a postdoctoral research fellow at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. His main research interests include knowledge graph reasoning, knowledge retrieval for large models, trustworthy intelligent services, and large-scale graph computing. He has published over 20 academic papers in top-tier conferences and journals, including TKDE, VLDB, ACL, WWW, and KDD. He also serves as a reviewer/program committee member for journals and conferences such as TITS, TKDD, WWW, KDD, NeurIPS, ICLR, and ICML.


      Personal HomePage： https://kynewang.github.io
