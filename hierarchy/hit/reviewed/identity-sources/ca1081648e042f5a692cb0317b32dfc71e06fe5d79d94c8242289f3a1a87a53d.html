  • Biography
      title

      Professor Tu Zhiying is a Professor and PhD Supervisor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Harbin Institute of Technology (Weihai). He serves as Vice Dean of the School, Executive Director of the Collaborative Innovation Center of the National Key Laboratory of Large-Scale Personalized Customization Systems and Technologies, and Deputy Director of the Shandong Key Laboratory of Digital Service Computing Technology and Systems. He also acts as Standing Committee Member and Deputy Secretary-General of the CCF Technical Committee on Service Computing, as well as Standing Director of the Shandong Computer Federation.


      His research focuses on cognitive intelligent software services and knowledge engineering. Over the past five years, as the principal investigator or key academic member, he has led and participated in seven national and provincial research projects, including grants from the National Natural Science Foundation of China, the National Key R&D Program of China, and the Shandong Provincial Key R&D Program.


      He has published more than 100 high-quality papers in top-tier international journals and conferences, such as IEEE TSC, IEEE TCC, IEEE TNNLS, ACM TOIT, SIGIR, ACL, ICML, ACM MM, and IEEE ICWS. He has received 6 Best Paper Awards at leading service computing conferences including ICWS and SCC. His academic outputs include two monographs, over 30 authorized national invention patents, 2 national standards and 3 association standards.


      His research has been recognized with multiple prestigious awards: the Special Prize of the Shandong Provincial Science and Technology Progress Award (2023), the First Prize of the Shandong Provincial Science and Technology Progress Award (2022), the First Prize of the Wu Wenjun Artificial Intelligence Science and Technology Progress Award conferred by the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence (2020), and the Second Prize of the Science and Technology Progress Award from the Chinese Institute of Electronics (2022).
