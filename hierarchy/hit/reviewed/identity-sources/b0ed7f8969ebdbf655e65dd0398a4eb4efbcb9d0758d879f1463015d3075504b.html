  • Basic Information
      title

      Ge Jingquan is an Associate Professor (research track), Doctoral Supervisor, and National Young Talent at the School of Computing, Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT). He received his Bachelor's degree from Ocean University of China in 2008, and his Master's and Doctoral degrees from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2016 and 2020, respectively. From 2020 to 2022, he was a postdoctoral researcher at Southern University of Science and Technology, and from 2022 to 2026, he was a postdoctoral researcher at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. He is currently working at the Computer Network and Information Security Research Center, School of Computing, Harbin Institute of Technology. His main research areas are LLM security, LLM-based system and software applications, hardware and software co-design for systems, software and encryption security. He has published over twenty academic papers in journals and conferences such as TCAD, TOSEM, TIFS, ESORICS, DATE, ICCD, and AsiaCCS, and serves as a reviewer/program committee member for journals and conferences such as TDSC, ACSAC, JSA, UIC, TJSC, and Cybersecurity.


      My personal homepage: https://gejingquan.github.io/
