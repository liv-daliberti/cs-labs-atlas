  • Basic Information
      名称

      翟德明，现任哈尔滨工业大学计算学部副教授、博士生导师。分别于2007年、2009年、2014年从哈工大计算机学院获得本科、硕士、博士学位。本科和硕士均以最高学生荣誉毕业，博士期间获得教育部博士研究生学术新人奖。2011年，在香港科技大学从事访问研究半年。2012年，在宾夕法尼亚大学GRASP实验室任访问学者。2015年，在日本国家信息学研究所担任特任研究员。研究兴趣为机器学习、多媒体分析、计算机视觉，研究成果发表于IEEE Trans. Image Processing、IEEE Trans. Multimedia、IEEE Trans. Industrial Electric、ACM Tomm、ICML、ICLR、CVPR、ACM Multimedia等国际顶级期刊和会议上。作为负责人主持国家自然科学基金面上与青年项目。



      招收硕士和博士研究生，研究方向：机器学习，数据挖掘，以及在计算机视觉和图像处理方面的应用，感兴趣的同学请与我联系。谢谢！

  • 基本信息
      title

      Dr. Deming Zhai is currently an associate professor, PHD advisor with the Department of Computer Science, Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT), Harbin, China. She received the B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. (Hons.) degrees in computer science from HIT in 2007, 2009, and 2014, respectively. In 2008, she joined the Joint Research and Development Lab (JDL), Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing, as a research assistant. In 2011, she spent half a year in Hong Kong University of Science and Technology as a visiting student. From March of 2012, she is with GRASP Laboratory, University of Pennsylvania, PA, USA, as a visiting scholar. In 2015, she worked as a project researcher at National Institute of Informatics (NII), Tokyo, Japan for one year. Her research interests include machine learning, data mining and their applications in computer vision and image processing.
