      名称

      丁小欧，哈尔滨工业大学计算学部副教授、博士生导师。于哈尔滨工业大学获得本科、博士学位。中国计算机学会（CCF) 数据库专委执行委员、CCF数据治理发展委员会执行委员、入选2025年黑龙江省青托工程项目、黑龙江省计算机学会数据科学与大数据技术专委会秘书长。研究方向为大数据治理、数据质量管理、面向机器学习的高质量数据构建、工业物联网时序数据治理与分析等，在IEEE TKDE、VLDB、ICDE、CIKM等数据科学领域国际重要期刊和会议上发表论文40余篇、在《软件学报》、《大数据》等国内高水平期刊上发表论文10余篇，参与编写专著4部(译著计算机科学丛书《数据挖掘概念与技术(第4版)》(第一作者))、获得中国发明专利8项、软件著作权6项。担任数据科学领域顶级会议VLDB 2024-2026出版主席、多个CCF A/B类会议程序委员会委员等。主持国家自然科学基金面上项目、青年项目、国家重大专项项目子课题、重点研发计划项目子课题、黑龙江省自然科学基金优秀青年项目、CCF-华为胡杨林数据库专项项目、中国博士后面上项目等。获2025年中国人工智能学会“吴文俊人工智能科学技术奖” 技术发明奖二等奖（第四）、中国商业联合会科学技术奖技术发明一等奖（排名第三）。以第一作者/第一完成人，获得CCF计算机应用创新产品奖一等奖（2024）、ACM China哈尔滨分会新星奖（2023）、领跑者5000——中国精品科技期刊顶尖论文奖（2024、2022）、《软件学报》2022年、2023年高影响力论文奖等。

  • Basic Information
      title

      Xiaoou Ding is an Associate Professor and Doctoral Supervisor in the Department of Computing at Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT). She received both her Bachelor's and Doctoral degrees from HIT. She currently serves as an Executive Member of the Database Special Committee of the China Computer Federation (CCF), an Executive Member of the CCF Data Governance Development Committee. Her research interests include big data governance, data quality management, high-quality data construction for machine learning, and time-series data governance and analysis. She has published over 30 papers in international journals and conferences in the data science field, including IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering (TKDE), VLDB, ICDE, and CIKM, as well as over 10 papers in high-level domestic journals such as the Journal of Software and Big Data Research in China. She has co-authored 3 monographs, obtained 8 Chinese invention patents, and 6 software copyrights.

      She has presided over numerous research projects, including the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) General Program, NSFC Youth Program, sub-project of the National Key R&D Program, Heilongjiang Provincial Natural Science Foundation Excellent Youth Program, CCF-Huawei Poplar Forest Database Special Project, and China Postdoctoral Science Foundation Project.

      As the first/corresponding author, she has received the First Prize of CCF Computer Application Innovation Product Award (2024), ACM China Harbin Chapter Rising Star Award (2023), Leader 5000 - Top Paper Award of China's Elite Scientific Journals (2024, 2022), and High-Impact Paper Awards from the Journal of Software (2022, 2023).
