      名称

      高岩，博士，哈尔滨工业大学计算学部教授、博士生导师，国家级高层次青年人才。2020年获哈尔滨工业大学计算机应用技术专业工学博士学位；2022—2024年于宾夕法尼亚大学从事博士后研究；2024—2026年于哈佛大学从事研究科学家工作；现任职于哈尔滨工业大学计算学部生物信息学研究中心。


      主要从事生物信息学算法、计算生物学、群体遗传学及生物大数据分析等方向研究，长期致力于高通量测序数据分析方法与计算工具研发。相关研究成果发表于 Cell、Nature、Nature Biotechnology、Nature Communications 等国际顶级期刊，论文总引用量超过3000次。


      现主持国家自然科学基金优秀青年科学基金项目（海外）和青年科学基金项目（C类），并担任黑龙江省重点研发计划课题负责人。


      担任中国计算机学会（CCF）生物信息学专业委员会委员，并担任 Nature Communications、The Innovation、Science Bulletin、Nucleic Acids Research、Briefings in Bioinformatics 等期刊审稿人。

  • Brief introduction
      title

      Dr. Yan Gao is a Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor in the Faculty of Computing at Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT) and a recipient of a national-level young talent program. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Applied Technology from HIT in 2020. From 2022 to 2024, he conducted postdoctoral research at the University of Pennsylvania, and from 2024 to 2026, he worked as a Research Scientist at Harvard University. He is currently affiliated with the Bioinformatics Research Center, Faculty of Computing, HIT.


      His research focuses on bioinformatics algorithm development, computational biology, population genetics, and large-scale biological data analysis, with particular emphasis on developing computational methods and tools for high-throughput sequencing data. His research has been published in leading journals, including Cell, Nature, Nature Biotechnology, and Nature Communications, and his publications have received more than 3,000 citations.


      He currently serves as Principal Investigator of the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) National High-Level Young Talents (Overseas) and Young Scientists Fund (Category C), and as a subproject leader under the Key R&D Program of Heilongjiang Province.


      He currently serves as a member of the Bioinformatics Technical Committee of the China Computer Federation (CCF) and as a reviewer for journals including Nature Communications, The Innovation, Science Bulletin, Nucleic Acids Research, and Briefings in Bioinformatics.
