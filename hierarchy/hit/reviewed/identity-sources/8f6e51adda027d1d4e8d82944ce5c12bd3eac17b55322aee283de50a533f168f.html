  • 简历
      名称


      苏统华， 2008年毕业于哈尔滨工业大学计算机科学与技术学院，获工学博士学位；之后进入中国科学院自动化研究所模式识别国家重点实验室，从事博士后研究工作。主要瞄准下一代人工智能科学问题，基于深度学习与异构计算等核心技术，聚焦开展图文多模态媒体生成与安全、大模型计算架构与服务优化等前沿研究与应用。主持完成国家级项目30余项，出版19本著作或译作，在TNNLS、Pattern Recognition、CVPR、ICCV、AAAI等顶刊顶会发表论文90余篇，获省科技进步二等奖1项。率先开展手写中文文本的研究工作，建立领域内首款手写中文库（HIT-MW库），该库为国内外200余家科研院所采用，出版手写汉字识别领域首本英文专著（德国施普林格出版社），获得手写汉字识别竞赛的2项世界冠军。自主研发大规模深度学习平台，成果转化到试卷全智能批改、手机手写输入法、内容生成、具身智能、工业检测等工业级产品，支撑了考试场景的智能化变革。


      获教育部-华为“智能基座”优秀教师、获聘首批华为昇腾领军人物（全国14人）、首批华为最具价值教师（全球18人），担任昇腾 CANN 技术管理委员会委员（高校代表2人）、担任MindSpore技术治理委员会成员（全球14人）、华为昇腾专家，连续4年评为全国最佳GPU教育工作者。

  • 研究领域
      名称

      主要研究方向:

      瞄准下一代人工智能关键问题，基于深度学习与异构计算等核心技术，聚焦开展多模态可持续交互大模型、多模态媒体生成与安全、大模型计算架构与服务优化等前沿研究与应用。团队的研究成果给出几个应用场景予以呈现：


      【基于“世界链”的VLA具身智能技术】提出“世界链” VLA新范式，将世界模型的时间推理能力与潜在动作的紧凑表示优势进行统一，发表于CVPR‘26。

      【动态梯度引导的多模态持续学习理论与技术】针对因梯度精度桎梏带来的灾难性遗忘难题，利用历史最优参数的几何指向性来补偿梯度，结合“方向向量尺度匹配”、“回放梯度融合”以及“伯努利随机注入”三重机制，解决纯回放方法的梯度偏差问题，灵活平衡了模型在新旧任务间的性能权衡，发表于CVPR‘26。

      【面向工业缺陷检测的投影阵列高速高精度三维重建技术】原创提出多投影系统的高速标定与高效自适应融合方案，在84ms内实现了98.2%的点云完整率和8.1μm的高度精度，应用于多个产线。

      【数字人 & 教育视频生成多Agent】基于原创说话人视频生成Agent，结合翻译 Agent、TTS Agent、标记 Agent、视频合成 Agent实现讲授课程内容的智能视频生成方案，应用于跨语言MOOC制作场景。


      【扮演大模型计算底座的AI基础软件】构建了由AsNumpy、PyAsc、OpenBOAT和AscendOpTest组成的开源AI基础软件栈，支撑大模型的计算底座，这些软件随CANN工具包发行。

      课程1：GPU计算（智能基座金课，牵头）

      本课程是计算机大类/软件工程大类的一门专业课程，目的是使学生了解GPU/AI处理器的基本设计理念，理解GPU/AI处理器计算模型，掌握面向GPU和AI处理器的软件设计、实现和调优技术， 培养学生面向实际计算密集型问题的并行处理器程序设计思维和能力。课程具体目标如下：

      理论课程目标T：

      掌握大规模处理器程序设计理论、思想与方法。具体而言：

      T1：掌握GPU计算和AI处理器的抽象模型、线程映射机制

      T2：GPU和AI处理器程序设计基本原理、语言要素和开发过程

      T3：理解基于GPU和AI处理器的常用并行算法思想

      实践课程目标P：

      具有利用GPU和AI处理器开发复杂大规模处理器软件系统的能力。具体而言：

      P1：利用GPU和AI处理器对串行程序进行并行化和调优的能力

      P2：具有使用这些算法解决实际问题的能力

      素质目标A：

      针对复杂的计算密集型工程问题，考虑社会、健康、安全、法律、文化以及环境等因素，开发、选择与使用恰当的技术、资源、现代工程工具和信息技术工具，进行程序正确性验证和性能的调优。

      本门课程意在提升学生的复杂工程问题分析能力、系统架构设计能力、典型软件系统开发及维护能力、面向性能的软件系统开发及优化能力等，将为学生进入软件企业工作和从事软件工程领域科研工作奠定良好的技术基础。课程具体目标如下：

      理论课程目标T：

      掌握复杂软件系统架构设计与分析相关的思想与方法。具体而言：

      T1：掌握复杂软件架构建模方法，尤其是4+1视图建模方法

      T2：掌握经典软件架构设计风格，尤其是事件驱动系统风格和MVC风格

      T3：掌握面向高性能、高可用、高扩展等非功能需求的架构设计方法及中间件技术

      实践课程目标P：

      具有使用主流软件中间件开发复杂软件系统的能力；能够设计和开发面向复杂软件系统的的核心中间件。具体而言：

      P1：具有复杂软件系统的架构设计能力，并能够通过软件架构文档开展架构分析、评估、交流与讨论。

      title

      Main research directions:

      Targeting the key issues of the next generation of artificial intelligence, based on core technologies such as deep learning and heterogeneous computing, we focus on conducting cutting-edge research and applications in areas such as multimodal sustainable interaction large models, multimodal media generation and security, as well as large model computing architecture and performance optimization. The team's research achievements are presented through several application scenarios:


      1. The "World Chain"-based VLA Embodied Intelligence Technology. We propose a new paradigm of "World Chain" VLA, which unifies the temporal reasoning capability of the world model with the compact representation advantage of potential actions. It was published at CVPR '26.

      2. Theory and Technology of Dynamic Gradient-Guided Multimodal Continuous Learning. Addressing the catastrophic forgetting problem caused by the limitations of gradient accuracy, this theory utilizes the geometric directionality of historical optimal parameters to compensate for gradients. Combined with the triple mechanisms of "directional vector scale matching", "replay gradient fusion", and "Bernoulli random injection", it solves the gradient bias problem of pure replay methods, flexibly balancing the performance trade-off between new and old tasks. This work was published at CVPR '26.

      3. High-Speed and High-Precision 3D Reconstruction Technology for Industrial Defect Detection Using Projection Arrays. This technology innovatively proposes a high-speed calibration and efficient adaptive fusion scheme for multi-projection systems, achieving a point cloud completeness rate of 98.2% and a height accuracy of 8.1μm within 84ms. It has been applied to multiple production lines.



      4. Digital Human & Educational Video Generation Multi-Agent. We implemented an intelligent video generation solution through generation agents based on original speaker videos, and combine translation agents, TTS agents, tagging agents, and video synthesis agents for teaching course content, which is applied to cross-language MOOC production scenarios.

      5. AI Basic Software Serving as the Computing Foundation for Large Models. We have established an open-source AI basic software stack, comprising AsNumpy, PyAsc, OpenBOAT, and AscendOpTest, to support the computing foundation of large models. These software components are distributed alongside the CANN toolkit.

      Course 1: GPU Computing (a golden course on the intelligent base, Principle Instructor)

      This course is a specialized course within the computer science/software engineering category. Its aim is to enable students to understand the basic design concepts of GPU/AI processors, comprehend the computational models of GPU/AI processors, and master the software design, implementation, and tuning techniques for GPU and AI processors. It cultivates students' thinking and ability in parallel processor programming for practical computationally intensive problems. The specific objectives of the course are as follows:

      Theoretical course objectives T:

      Master the theory, ideas, and methods of large-scale processor programming. Specifically:

      T1: Master the abstract model and thread mapping mechanism of GPU computing and AI processors

      T2: Basic principles, language elements, and development process of GPU and AI processor programming

      T3: Understand the common parallel algorithm concepts based on GPU and AI processors

      Practical course objective P:

      Possess the capability to develop complex large-scale processor software systems utilizing GPUs and AI processors. Specifically:

      P1: The capability to parallelize and optimize serial programs utilizing GPUs and AI processors

      P2: Ability to apply these algorithms to solve practical problems

      Competency Objective A:

      For complex computationally intensive engineering problems, it is essential to consider factors such as society, health, safety, law, culture, and the environment. Appropriate technologies, resources, modern engineering tools, and information technology tools should be developed, selected, and utilized to verify program correctness and optimize performance.


      Course 2: Software Architecture and Middleware (Provincial First-Class Course, Principle Instructor)

      This course is one of the core courses in the software engineering/computer science major. Its goal is to enable students to further learn the analysis methods for complex software engineering problems and master the development methods for complex software systems based on architectural thinking, on the basis of understanding the software engineering process and initially mastering software development methods.This course aims to enhance students' abilities in analyzing complex engineering problems, designing system architectures, developing and maintaining typical software systems, and developing and optimizing performance-oriented software systems. It will lay a solid technical foundation for students to enter software companies and engage in research work in the field of software engineering. The specific objectives of the course are as follows:

      Theoretical course objectives T:

      Master the ideas and methods related to the design and analysis of complex software system architecture. Specifically:

      T1: Master complex software architecture modeling methods, especially the 4+1 view modeling method

      T2: Master classic software architecture design styles, especially event-driven system style and MVC style

      T3: Master the architecture design methods and middleware technologies tailored for non-functional requirements such as high performance, high availability, and high scalability

      Practical course objective P:

      Possess the capability to develop complex software systems using mainstream software middleware; be able to design and develop core middleware for complex software systems. Specifically:

      P1: Possess the ability to design the architecture of complex software systems and conduct architecture analysis, evaluation, communication, and discussion through software architecture documents.

      P2: Ability to develop complex software systems based on mainstream open-source middleware tools

      P3: Ability to develop simple middleware systems

      Competency Objective A:

      Possess an engineering mindset and a systematic perspective, and develop the ability to employ suitable models to articulate and analyze intricate software engineering challenges.


      Course 3: Artificial Intelligence System Architecture (a core graduate course in the field of artificial intelligence led by the Ministry of Education, Principle Instructor)

      This course adopts a holistic perspective that integrates both soft and hard aspects to examine the various components of artificial intelligence systems. It serves as a crucial methodology and technology supporting the development of today's large models. Through studying this course, students will be able to establish a knowledge system for the design and application of artificial intelligence systems, understand the current research and practical progress of artificial intelligence system architecture, and closely integrate with engineering applications. They will master the basic skills of artificial intelligence application development and enhance their ability to apply these methods to solve practical problems. This lays a theoretical foundation for future research in fields related to artificial intelligence system design.

      The course covers the hardware and software architecture of artificial intelligence systems. It includes the hardware principles of artificial intelligence processors, artificial intelligence compiler technology, artificial intelligence model inference technology, distributed cluster heterogeneous computing and scheduling algorithms, artificial intelligence frameworks, and large model training. It incorporates several case studies, proposing specific solutions from the perspective of practical projects and experimental research. Additionally, it tracks the latest technologies and literature through classroom discussions, keeping abreast of international cutting-edge research. Project experiments are conducted to enhance students' practical abilities.
