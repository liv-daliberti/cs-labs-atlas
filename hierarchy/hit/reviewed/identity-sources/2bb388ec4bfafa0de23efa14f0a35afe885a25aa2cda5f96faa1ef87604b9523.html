      名称



      赵京东，男，汉族，1977年10月生，中共党员。研究员（正高二级，长聘二岗1级），博士生导师，机器人技术与系统全国重点实验室执行主任，哈尔滨工业大学机电工程学院党委书记，国家高层次人才计划入选者，龙江学者特聘教授。

      主要研究方向为空间机器人技术和生物机电一体化技术。主持国家自然科学基金项目（重大研究计划重点项目/培育项目/面上项目/青年项目）、国家部委项目，863重大专项子项目、民用航天预研项目、921三期预研、市科技创新人才研究专项高层次人才项目、国重自主课题、博士后特别资助等，作为核心骨干参与国家自然科学基金基础科学中心项目、重点项目、教育部学科先导计划项目、863重大专项和重点项目、民用航天预研/基础科研等10余项。发表SCI/EI论文100余篇，编写专著1部，参编专著2部，授权国家发明专利53项。获国家技术发明二等奖1项（排2），部委技术发明一等奖1项（排1），科技创新团队奖1项，教育部技术发明一等奖1项，黑龙江省技术发明一等奖3项（1项排1），二等奖2项。

      个人信息 / Biographical Information
      Zhao Jingdong, born in October 1977 . Research Fellow, doctoral supervisor, serves as Party Secretary of the School of Mechatronics Engineering at Harbin Institute of Technology, and Executive Director of the State Key Laboratory of Robotics and Systems. He is the recipient of the National High-Level Talent Program and the Longjiang Scholar Distinguished Professor.

      His primary research focuses on space robotics and biomechatronics technologies. He has presided over National Natural Science Foundation Programs (Major Research Plan Key Program, Cultivation Program, General Program, and Youth Program), national ministry programs, 863 Major Program sub-program, municipal science and technology innovation talent research special program for high-level talent, independent program of state key laboratory, postdoctoral special funding, etc., and as the key member participated in over 10 programs including the National Natural Science Foundation under the Basic Science Center Program, Key programs, 863 Major Programs and Key Programs, etc. He has published over 100 papers and one monograph, participated in compiling two monographs, and granted 45 national invention patents. His awards include a second prize of the State Technological Invention Award (second contributor), a Science and Technology Innovation Team Award, a first prize of Technology Invention Award of Ministry of Education, there first prizes and two second prizes of Technology Innovation Award of Heilongjiang Province.
