  • Biographical Information
      title
      Tianyi ZANG Ph.D.

      Professor with Tenure and Ph.D. Supervisor in Faculty of Computing at Harbin Institute of Technology
      Ph.D. Supervisor at Pengcheng National Laboratory
      Flexible Leadership Talent under Kunlun Talent Program of Qinghai Province

      Prof. Zang serves as a grant review panelist/expert for the National Key R&D Program of China and the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC). Prior to HIT, he held research positions at the University of Oxford in UK, and the National Institute of High Performance Computing in Singapore respectively. He was honored as a National Outstanding Individual Among Returned Overseas Chinese, which is a national award in China.

      His research is concerned with generative models and algorithms, autonomous learning models and algorithms, agent AI and distributed systems, AI for science/engineering, and autonomous platform systems with focusing on drug AI, materials AI, and agentic precision medicine/healthcare.

      He has published more than 130 papers in top-level journals and conferences, such as BIB, Bioinformatics, Genome Biology, PNAS, BIBM conference, and so on. He holds more than 30 national invention patents and software copyrights in China.

      Prof. Zang has won multiple competitive research grants from various grant bodies and programs, including National Key R&D Program, National High-Tech R&D Program，National Science and Technology Major Projects，National Science and Technology Support Program, NSFC, 'Unveiling the List and Appointing the Leader' initiative, and so on.
