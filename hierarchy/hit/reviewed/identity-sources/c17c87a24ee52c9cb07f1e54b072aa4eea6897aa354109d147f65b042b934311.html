  • 情感计算组代表性成果
      名称

      1. “巧板”（http://qiaoban.hit-scir.com）

      首个面向K12中小学生群体的情感陪伴大模型对话系统。该系统主要面向K12中小学生及其家长群体提供日常情感陪伴，具有共情对话、情绪疏导、角色化陪伴以及中国优秀传统文化熏陶等多个功能。目前，“巧板”的相关技术已融入了科大讯飞学习机的 “AI心理伙伴”系统中。此外，“巧板”的相关技术也与华为小艺等进行了深入合作，显著提升了这些平台在情感识别和个性化互动方面的能力。

      2. 情绪地图（http://qx.8wss.com）

      情绪地图旨在通过对海量社会媒体数据的分析、挖掘和可视化，围绕社会焦点事件进行事件发现、情绪分析和深层透视。该成果的相关技术多次在国内外知名技术评测中名列前茅，并得到了国家 973 计划、国家自然科学基金重点项目以及多家知名企业的项目资助，有较大的经济价值和社会效益。

      在新冠肺炎病毒肆虐期间，启动了“新冠肺炎疫情微博情绪地图”（http://qx.8wss.com/qx-virus-develop-private/）的科研攻关，这是国内高校打造的信息含量最大的疫情分析系统之一。该系统旨在自动处理每日产生的数以千万级的微博数据，抽取全国的热点事件、展示全国及各省民众的情绪分布，并追踪民众的情绪趋势。面向微博的舆情分析系统研究成果提交到了中央网信办，中央网信办对此成果表示认可，并提供证明表示“这些技术对网上舆情进行了深度分析研判，为我中心工作提供了有力支持”。

      Zhao Yanyan is a Tenured Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor at the Faculty of Computing, Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT). She is recognized as a high-level talent at the provincial and ministerial level and has served as a Visiting Scholar at the University of California, Berkeley. Currently, she serves as a committee member of both the Emotion Computing and the Social Media Processing Committees under the Chinese Information Processing Society of China (CIPSC). She has also held prominent roles such as Area Chair and Local Chair at numerous top-tier domestic and international conferences.

      Her outstanding contributions have been recognized with several prestigious awards, including the First Prize of the Ministry of Education's Excellent Scientific Research Achievement in Science and Technology, two First Prizes for Scientific and Technological Progress in Heilongjiang Province, the First Prize of the Wu Wenjun AI Technology Progress Award, two Second Prizes for Scientific and Technological Progress in Heilongjiang Province, and the Best Youth Science and Technology Achievement Award from the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence.

      Professor Zhao's primary research interests lie in Large Language Models (LLMs), Affective Computing, and Social Computing. In recent years, she has presided over and participated in numerous major projects, including those funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, the Ministry of Science and Technology's National Key R&D Program, and National Laboratory projects. Furthermore, she actively engages in industry-academia collaboration with leading domestic enterprises such as Huawei, iFlytek, Du Xiaoman, Tencent, and Seres.

      She leads a team that has developed "Qiaoban" (http://qiaoban.hit-scir.com), the first LLM-based conversational system designed to provide emotional companionship for K-12 students. She has also authored three monographs: Emotional Conversational Robots, Affective Computing, and Social Media Mining. To date, she has published over 100 academic papers in her field, including works presented at top international conferences like ACL, NeurIPS (NIPS), and AAAI.
