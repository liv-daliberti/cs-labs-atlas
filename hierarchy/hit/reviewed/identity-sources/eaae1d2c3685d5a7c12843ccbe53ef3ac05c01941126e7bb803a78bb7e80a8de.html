  • 基本信息
      名称



      何慧，女，汉族。博士，教授，博士生导师。中国计算机学会会员， ACM会员，IEEE&IEEE Computer 会员，计算机通信学会高级会员。2015年评为博士生导师，2016年晋升为教授。2017年1月至2018年1月在美国加州大学戴维斯分校访问一年。主要从事网络与信息安全、大数据安全、AI安全、认知安全、车联网及安全、边缘计算等方面的研究。共承担国家网络信息安全工程重点项目、国家自然科学基金、国家重点研发计划、863重点等其他省部委项目近等近30项； 2024年获省教学二等奖一项，校教学一等奖两项。2021获得黑龙江省科技进步二等奖，2020年获得黑龙江省高校科学技术一等奖，2010年指导学生参加全国信息安全竞赛获一等奖。 2009年黑龙江省科技进步二等奖等。近五年，在TIFS、ITC，TKDE、WWW、ACL、FGCS、电子学报、软件学报、计算机学报、通信学报等国内外刊物与会议发表论文近200篇，其中顶会顶刊通信或一作10余篇，SCI索引50余篇，EI索引30余篇。共获得国家授权发明专利30余项，软件著作权多项，专著四部。教育部学位中心评审专家，中央网络安全和信息化领导小组办公室与国家互联网信息办公室的网络安全领域专家，国家计算机网络与信息安全技术研究专项评审专家，黑龙江省网络专委会秘书长等。全国大学生竞赛2009-2024年度的网评专家等；担任国内外期刊编辑、审稿人等。

  • My Short CV
      名称


      Dr. Hui He is currently an Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor in the School of Computer Science and Technology, Harbin Institute of Technology, China. He received his B.Eng, M.Eng and Ph.D. degree of Engineering in computer science and technology in 1997, 1999 and 2006 respectively from Harbin Institute of Technology.Main research areas include network and information security, big data security, AI security, cognitive security, Internet of Vehicles and its security, and edge computing. Has undertaken nearly 30 national and ministerial-level projects, including key projects in national network and information security engineering, National Natural Science Foundation projects, National Key R&D Program projects, and 863 Key Projects. In 2024, received one second prize in provincial teaching awards and two first prizes in university teaching awards. In 2021, awarded the Second Prize of Heilongjiang Provincial Science and Technology Progress Award; in 2020, received the First Prize of Heilongjiang Provincial Higher Education Institutions Science and Technology Award; in 2010, guided students to win the first prize in the National Information Security Competition. In 2009, awarded the Second Prize of Heilongjiang Provincial Science and Technology Progress Award. Over the past five years, has published nearly 200 papers in domestic and international journals and conferences, including TIFS, ITC, TKDE, WWW, ACL, FGCS,etc.
