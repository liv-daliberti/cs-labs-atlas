  • BIOGRAPHY
      title

      Dr. Xiaocheng Feng is a Professor (selected for the HIT Young Talents Program) and Ph.D. supervisor at the Faculty of Computing, Harbin Institute of Technology. He serves as the Vice Dean of the School of Artificial Intelligence and Assistant to the Director of the Heilongjiang Provincial Key Laboratory of Chinese Information Processing. He is recognized as a National High-level Young Talent.

      His research interests primarily focus on Natural Language Processing, Text Generation, and Machine Translation. He has published over 70 papers in top-tier venues (CCF A/B), including ACL, AAAI, IJCAI, TKDE, and TOIS. According to Google Scholar, his publications have garnered over 14,000 citations. Notably, two of his papers were recognized as "Google Scholar Classic Papers 2020," and one was ranked among the "Paper Digest EMNLP 2020 Top Ten Most Influential Papers." He has served as a Senior Program Committee (SPC) or PC member for prestigious conferences such as NeurIPS, ICML, AAAI, IJCAI, and ACL. Furthermore, he holds several key leadership roles, including Dual-Appointed Scholar at Peng Cheng Laboratory, Vice Director of the CIPS Youth Working Committee, and Chair of CCF YOCSEF Harbin (2023-2024).

      He was selected for the 6th Young Elite Scientist Sponsorship Program by the China Association for Science and Technology (CAST). His distinguished honors include the Best Doctoral Dissertation Award from the Chinese Information Processing Society of China (CIPS), the First Prize of the Ministry of Education Technology Innovation Award, the First Prize of the Wu Wenjun AI Science and Technology Award, and multiple Heilongjiang Provincial Science and Technology Progress Awards (First and Second Class). He is also the recipient of the 2023 CCF-NLP Young Scientist Award and the 2022 WAIC Yunfan Award.As a Principal Investigator (PI), he has spearheaded numerous high-level research initiatives, including the National Natural Science Foundation for Excellent Young Scholars (Category B), sub-projects of the "Science and Technology Innovation 2030" New Generation AI Major Project, and key projects from National Laboratories. His portfolio also encompasses General and Youth Programs of the NSFC, the Heilongjiang Provincial Key R&D Program, the Provincial Outstanding Youth Fund, and the MSRA Collaborative Research Program. Furthermore, he fosters long-term strategic research collaborations with world-class technology leaders, including Huawei, Tencent, iFLYTEK, and Microsoft.
