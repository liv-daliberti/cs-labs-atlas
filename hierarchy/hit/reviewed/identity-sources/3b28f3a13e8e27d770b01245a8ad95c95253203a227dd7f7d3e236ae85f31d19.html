    • Bio
        title

        Dr. Donghua Yang is an associate professor at Faculty of Computing, Harbin Institute of Technology, China. He received his B.S., M.E. and Ph.D. degrees from Harbin Institute of Technology, China in 1999, 2003 and 2008, respectively. His research interests include database systems and big data analytics. He is the principal investigators of 3 research projected funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC). He has published more than 50 papers in the refereed journals and conferences including VLDB Journal, IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering, Information Systems, IEEE Internet of Things Journal, Knowledge-Based Systems, World Wide Web, KDD and DASFAA.
