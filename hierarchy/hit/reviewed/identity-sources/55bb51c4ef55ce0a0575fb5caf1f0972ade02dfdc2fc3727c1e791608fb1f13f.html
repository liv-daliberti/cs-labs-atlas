  • 个人简介
      名称

      晁国清，博士，研究员，特聘教授，博士生导师，斯坦福全球2%顶尖科学家。中国计算机学会(CCF)杰出会员、CCF 人工智能与模式识别专委会执行委员、CCF服务计算专委会执行委员，中国人工智能学会(CAAI)会员、CAAI智能服务专委会委员，中国自动化学会(CAA)会员、CAA模式识别与机器智能专委会委员，山东省人工智能学会常务理事，YOCSEF青岛2024-2025主席。国家自然科学基金评议专家，教育部学位中心论文评审专家，山东省科技专家库研发专家，青岛科技局专家库技术研发专家。研究兴趣包括机器学习、数据挖掘、智慧医疗、元宇宙、服务计算与工程，先后在美国康涅狄格大学，西北大学以及新加坡管理大学做博士后和Research Scientist。近年来主持或主研包括国家自然科学基金面上项目(2项)、国家重点研发计划、山东省重点研发项目课题子课题等近十项。研究成果主要发表在TKDE, TNNLS, TCSVT, TMM, Information Fusion, PR， Information Sciences, KBS, Applied Soft Computing, ICML,KDD, CVPR, ACM MM, AAAI, IJCAI, BIBM等国际顶级期刊会议，含高被引论文2篇和最佳专题论文1篇。担任SCI期刊PR和MAKE的副编辑或编委，Applied Intelligence和Neural Processing Letters 的客座执行编辑，担 任ICML, KDD, NeurIPS, ACM MM, AAAI, IJCAI, ICLR, JMLR, TPAMI,TNNLS, TKDE, TFS, T-Cybernetics,TSC, PR等二十余个国际顶级会议期刊审稿人。曾获上海市自然科学二等奖。

  • Basic Information
      title

      Guoqing Chao, Ph.D., Professor, Doctoral Supervisor, and Stanford Top 2% World's Most Cited Scientist. He is a Senior Member of the China Computer Federation (CCF), Executive Committee Member of CCF Technical Committee on Artificial Intelligence and Pattern Recognition, Executive Committee Member of CCF Technical Committee on Service Computing, Member of the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence (CAAI), Committee Member of CAAI Technical Committee on Intelligent Services, Member of the Chinese Association of Automation (CAA), Committee Member of CAA Technical Committee on Pattern Recognition and Machine Intelligence, Executive Director of Shandong Association of Artificial Intelligence, and Chair of YOCSEF Qingdao for 2024-2025. He also serves as a Reviewer for the National Natural Science Foundation of China, Thesis Reviewer for the Degree Center of the Ministry of Education, R&D Expert of Shandong Provincial Science and Technology Expert Database, and Technical R&D Expert of Qingdao Science and Technology Bureau Expert Database. His research interests include machine learning, data mining, smart healthcare, metaverse, service computing and engineering. He previously worked as a Postdoctoral Fellow and Research Scientist at the University of Connecticut, Northwestern University, and Singapore Management University. In recent years, he has led or participated in nearly ten research projects, including General Projects of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, National Key Research and Development Program of China, and sub-projects of Shandong Provincial Key Research and Development Program. His research outcomes have been primarily published in top international journals and conferences such as TKDE, TNNLS, TCSVT, TMM, Information Fusion, Information Sciences, KBS, Applied Soft Computing, ICML, KDD, CVPR, ACM MM, AAAI, IJCAI, and BIBM, including two highly cited papers and one best special issue paper. He serves as a Guest Executive Editor for the SCI journals Applied Intelligence and Neural Processing Letters, and as a Reviewer for more than twenty top international conferences and journals, including ICML, KDD, NeurIPS, ACM MM, AAAI, IJCAI, ICLR, JMLR, TPAMI, TNNLS, TKDE, TFS, T-Cybernetics, TSC, and PR. He has received the Second Prize of Natural Science of Shanghai.
