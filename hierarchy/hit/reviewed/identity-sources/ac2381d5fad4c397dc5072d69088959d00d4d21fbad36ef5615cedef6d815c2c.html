      名称

      任宏伟，博士，计算学部副教授，神舟青年学者。2025年取得香港科技大学博士学位。现就职于多模态智能及应用研究中心。主要研究方向为类脑计算、事件相机、多模态融合以及表征学习，在TPAMI, CVPR, ICLR等会议期刊发表三十余篇学术论文，担任NeurIPS、ICLR领域主席，并长期在IJCV、CVPR、ICCV、NeurIPS、ICLR、ICML等期刊会议审稿人/程序委员会委员。曾获南方科技大学十佳毕业生，国际国内比赛一等奖若干。

      主要研究方向：类脑计算，事件相机，脉冲神经网络，多模态医疗

  • Basic Information
      title

      Hongwei Ren, Ph.D.Associate Professor, Faculty of Computing, Shenzhou Young Scholar. He is currently affiliated with the Research Center for Multimodal Intelligence and Applications (MAIA).


      Research Interests: Brain-inspired computing, event cameras, multimodal fusion, and representation learning.


      He has published over ten academic papers as the first/co-first author in top-tier journals and conferences, including IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI), Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR), and International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR). He serves as a reviewer or program committee member for prestigious venues such as International Journal of Computer Vision (IJCV), CVPR, International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV), Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), ICLR, and International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML). He also serves as Area Chair in ICLR.


      Awards & Honors:

      National Champion / First Prize, China Service Robot Competition (2018)

      Global First Prize, Innovation Track, Huawei ICT Competition (2021)



      Core Research Directions: Brain-inspired computing, event cameras, spiking neural networks, multimodal medical intelligence
