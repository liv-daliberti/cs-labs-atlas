      名称

      赵天意，教授、博士生导师、生物信息学系主任、生物信息技术研究院副院长。

      1992年出生于黑龙江省哈尔滨市，2018-2020年间赴哈佛大学进行联合培养。



      2020.12-2024.2 青年拔尖副教授

      2024.2至今 教授




      研究方向为单细胞数据分析算法、生物网络分析算法，旨在通过对深度学习、机器学习算法的创新突破解决生物学问题。主持国家重点研发计划课题1项、国家自然基金2项、黑龙江省重点研发计划2项、哈工大青年人才项目1项、点将-请缨项目1项、医工交叉项目1项。


      在计算学部招收硕士、博士生(计算机应用技术、智能科学)

      在医学与健康学院招收硕士、博士生(生物医学工程)

      报名学生需具备一定的编程计算能力

      title

      Tianyi Zhao, Professor, doctoral supervisor, Director of the Department of Bioinformatics, Vice President of the Institute of Bioinformatics.

      Born in Harbin, Heilongjiang Province in 1992, he went to Harvard University for joint training from 2018 to 2020.


      2020.12-2024.2 Associate Professor

      2024.2-now Professor


      His research focuses on single-cell data analysis algorithms and biological network analysis algorithms, aiming to address biological problems through innovative breakthroughs in deep learning and machine learning algorithms.He has led 1 National Key Research and Development Program project, 1 National Natural Science Foundation project, 1 Young Talent Project at Harbin Institute of Technology, and 1 interdisciplinary project in medical engineering.


      Recruitment:

      MSC and PhD candidates in the Department of Computing (Applied Computer Technology)

      Master and PhD students in Zhengzhou Research Institute(Electronic Information)

      MSC and PhD candidates in School of Medicine and Health (Biomedical Engineering)
