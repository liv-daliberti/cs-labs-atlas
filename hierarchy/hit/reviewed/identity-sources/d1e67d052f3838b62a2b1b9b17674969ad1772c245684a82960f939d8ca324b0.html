  • 教师简介
      名称

      王凯，博士，山东省高层次青年人才，哈工大威海校区教授/计算机学院院长助理、哈工大计算学部博士生导师


      研究概述：研究方向为网络与人工智能安全，主持国家自然科学基金、国家信息安全计划项目、国家重点研发计划子课题、工业互联网创新发展工程课题、山东省自然科学基金等各类科研项目，在IEEE TIFS、IEEE TDSC、IEEE TMC、IEEE TSC等CCF-A类、中科院一区、IEEE/ACM汇刊上以第一/通讯作者发表论文40余篇，学术引用3000余次，代表作多次入选ESI高被引论文，授权发明专利20余项，主研的安全工具列装国家公安部，获中国电子学会科技进步二等奖1项，指导学生获教育部榜单赛事全国特等奖、黑龙江省优秀毕业生等，担任国家工程研究中心外部专家、工信部重点实验室副主任委员、国家科技专家库专家、国家自然科学基金项目通讯评审专家、教育部学位论文评审专家、山东省科技专家库专家、中国电子学会网络空间安全专家委员会委员、山东计算机学会理事、中国计算机学会高级会员、AAAI 2027 PC member、CCF-A类领域顶刊审稿人等。


      学生培养：培养的研究生获评“黑龙江省优秀毕业生”、“哈尔滨工业大学优秀毕业生”、“哈尔滨工业大学优秀硕士学位论文”等称号，毕业去向主要在香港、北京、深圳等地，就业单位包括黑龙江省纪委、惠州检察院、中国科学院空天院、国家电网大数据中心、北京市产品质量监督检验研究院、华为（苏州）、小米通讯、顺丰科技、Laboratory for AI-Powered Financial Technologies Limited（香港）、TCL鸿鹄实验室等。


      招生信息：每年招收1～2名博士生以及1～3名硕士生

      要求申请者积极上进、责任心和团队意识强且具备扎实的理论基础、代码能力以及优秀的写作素养。其中，博士生申请还需满足的要求详见哈尔滨工业大学博士研究生“申请-考核”条件要求。

      招生学科：

      （2）电子信息（专业学位）—— 博士、硕士

  • Basic Academic Background
      title

      Kai Wang, Ph.D., Professor, Doctoral Supervisor, Shandong Provincial High-Level Young Talent, Assistant Dean of the School of Computer Science and Technology, Harbin Institute of Technology, Weihai.

      Research Directions

      Artificial Intelligence Security: Deepfake Detection, Trustworthy Large Language Models, etc.

      Network Intrusion Detection: In-Vehicle Network Intrusion Detection, etc.

      In recent years, Professor Wang has presided over more than 10 national and provincial-level projects, such as the National Natural Science Foundation of China, sub-projects of the National Key R&D Program, and the Shandong Provincial Natural Science Foundation. As the first author or corresponding author, he has published more than 40 high-impact academic papers in ACM/IEEE Transactions series, CCF-A category journals/conferences, and CAS (Chinese Academy of Sciences) Tier-1 journals. His representative works have been repeatedly selected as "ESI Highly Cited Papers". He has obtained 18 authorized invention patents, and the equipment, systems, and platforms developed under his leadership have been applied in many well-known domestic enterprises and public institutions. He was awarded the Second Prize of Science and Technology Progress by the China Institute of Electronics.

      Academic Affiliations

      AAAI 2027 PC member

      External Expert of the National Engineering Research Center for Mobile Special Networks

      Expert of the Ministry of Science and Technology of the People's Republic of China

      Thesis Review Expert of the Academic Degree Center of the Ministry of Education of the People's Republic of China

      Review Expert of the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC)

      Member of the Cyberspace Security Expert Committee of the China Electronics Society

      Senior Member of the China Computer Federation (CCF)

      Reviewer for Top CCF-A Journals and Conferences

      Student Cultivation

      His graduate students have received honors such as Outstanding Graduate of Heilongjiang Province, Outstanding Graduate of Harbin Institute of Technology, and Outstanding Master’s Thesis of Harbin Institute of Technology.

      Graduates mainly pursue careers in Hong Kong, Beijing, Shenzhen and other major cities, and are employed by well-known institutions and enterprises, including:Aerospace Information Research Institute (CAS), Big Data Center of State Grid, Beijing Product Quality Supervision and Inspection Institute, Huawei (Suzhou), Xiaomi Communications, SF Technology, Laboratory for AI-Powered Financial Technologies Limited (Hong Kong), TCL Honghu Laboratory, etc.

      Admission Information

      He recruits 1–2 PhD candidates and 1–3 master’s students annually.

      Ideal applicants shall be self-motivated, responsible, team-oriented, with solid theoretical foundation, proficient coding capability and excellent academic writing competence.

      For doctoral applicants, additional requirements shall be in accordance with the "Application-Assessment" criteria for doctoral students of Harbin Institute of Technology.

      Email: dr.wangkai@hit.edu.cn
