  • Basic Information
      title


      Dongyang Zhan, Associate Professor and Doctoral Advisor. He serves in the Faculty of Computing at Harbin Institute of Technology. His main research interests include system security (e.g., anomalous behavior analysis, cloud computing security, and operating system security), and he has published more than 20 papers in top journals and conferences such as USENIX Security, IEEE TDSC, TC, IEEE TSE, IEEE TSC, etc. He serves as an executive member of the CCF System Software Specialized Committee, a youth editorial board member of authoritative journals at home and abroad, such as the Journal of Information Security and Cybersecurity, and a reviewer of IEEE TDSC, IET Information Security, and other journals. He is the supervisor of the Lilac CTF team of Harbin Institute of Technology and the excellent instructor of the National College Student Information Security Contest.
