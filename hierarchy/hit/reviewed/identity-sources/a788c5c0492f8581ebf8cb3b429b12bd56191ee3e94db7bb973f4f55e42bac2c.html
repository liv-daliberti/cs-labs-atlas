      名称


      黄志伟，哈工大生命科学与技术学院教授、院长，生命科学中心主任。2008年从北京生命科学研究所柴继杰教授结构生物学实验室博士毕业，然后去哈佛大学免疫与感染疾病系 Laurie H. Glimcher教授免疫学实验室做博士后，2012年回国到哈工大建立实验室，研究人适应性免疫的分子机制，以及微生物免疫与新型基因编辑工具的开发。

      Dr. Zhiwei Huang, professor and dean of the School of Life Science and Technology and director of HIT Center for Life Sciences at Harbin Institute of Technology. After finishing his PhD thesis from the structural biology lab of Dr. Jijie Chai at National Institute of Biological Sciences in 2008, he then went to the immunological lab of Dr. Laurie H. Glimcher at Harvard University to receive his postdoctoral training. In March 2012, he joined the School and Life Science and Technology at Harbin Institute of Technology as a professor. His research mainly focuses on studying the molecular mechanisms of bacterial and human adaptive immune systems and developing novel genome editing tools.

      主要研究方向/Research areas

      采用整合的研究方法包括生物信息学、病毒学、免疫学、结构生物学以及小鼠遗传学等，从分子、细胞以及个体水平等多个层次以较全视野对适应性免疫系统以及病毒拮抗机制进行研究。研究方向包括：

      1. 研究适应性免疫细胞的免疫功能与分子机制。

      2. 研究细菌CRISPR-Cas系统以及噬菌体Anti-CRISPR系统的分子机制；设计高效的、特异性的新型基因编辑工具。

      3. 研究在生理和病理过程中人微生态系统中病毒、细菌的功能与机制。

      4. 解析免疫与感染性疾病发生过程中起关键作用的蛋白（复合物）的分子机制，并利用这些结构信息理性设计小分子药物治疗相关疾病。

      We use multi-disciplinary approaches including bioinformatics, single cell sequencing, immunology, structural biology, and mouse models from to study the molecular mechanisms of microbe and human adaptive immune systems, microbial dysbiosis in human diseases, how immune systems interaction with other systems in the body, and developing novel genome editing tools for the treatment of genetic disorders.
