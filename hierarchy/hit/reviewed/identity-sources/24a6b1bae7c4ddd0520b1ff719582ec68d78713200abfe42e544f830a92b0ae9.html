  • Biography
      title

      Songhao PIAO is Professor, Doctoral Advisor, and Director of the Intelligent Robotics Research Center at Harbin Institute of Technology. His research focuses on artificial intelligence, computer vision, robot perception and navigation, motion planning, multi-robot coordination, embedded systems, and human–robot interaction. He has led or participated in major projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, the 863 Program, and international collaborations with Samsung. He has published 2 monographs and over 100 peer-reviewed papers indexed by SCI/EI/ISTP.
