# MIT EECS Stage 1 source review — 2026-11-11

**Strict Stage 1 pass; zero waivers.** MIT's released legacy charter had no `[[departments]]`, so its prior Stage 1 result did not measure a department. This change defines two full-coverage Stage 1 units: EECS and CSAIL. CSAIL is a research laboratory, not a degree-granting department; it is a separate unit in the existing MIT listing plan and must be counted in the gate rather than silently omitted.

The official 2016 and 2020 EECS `people/` pages link to `people/faculty-advisors`. An exact Wayback query for that directory found 50 HTTP-200 captures covering every half-year from 2016 H1 through 2021 H2. Sampled 2016, 2020, and 2021 pages contain 165, 174, and 179 faculty/adviser rows respectively, with printed individual titles and no pager. Some names have no profile hyperlink; the parser keeps those named, titled rows without inventing a link.

After the site redesign, the EECS faculty-only role directory at `role/faculty/` has 27 exact 200 captures from 2021 H2 through 2026 H1, covering every half-year in 2022–2025. Its sampled 2022 page has 166 faculty cards with individual titles and profile links. The general `people/` page also has captures through 2026 H2, but mixes faculty with staff and is not a substitute for the faculty-only listing. Several later sample fetches remain pending or unusable; a CDX 200 alone is not a usable roster.

The two EECS parsers preserve printed names, titles, and profile URLs. The charter limits each EECS slot to its observed era. The pre-existing `listings/scripts/config_mit.py` also plans CSAIL PI directories, including the 2022+ filtered Solr endpoint; it takes precedence over charter slots in the legacy listing runner. Restartable job `32213936` completed the archive plan. CSAIL's 2016–2017 JSON, 2018–2021 cards, and complete 2022–2025 Solr responses now parse into dated named PI rows. The 2026 archived Solr response contained only 216 of its declared 309 entries and was rejected as incomplete. A robots-checked official live Solr response saved on 2026-11-11 contains all 132 of its declared 132 entries; the reviewed live-source manifest pins its exact URL, date, hash, and entry count.

The standard derivation has actual CSAIL PI rows in every year 2016–2026 (114, 114, 230, 236, 241, 248, 263, 262, 133, 130, 132; multiple half-year snapshots inflate some counts). `stage_gate.py mit 1` reports pass, open 0, waived 0 with both EECS and CSAIL included. The Stage 1 listing does not itself decide PI identity or appointment periods.
