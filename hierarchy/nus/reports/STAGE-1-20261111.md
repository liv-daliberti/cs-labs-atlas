# NUS Stage 1 source review — 2026-11-11

The Department of Computer Science has accepted official faculty rosters for at least one half of every year from 2016 through 2026. The 2016–21 faculty tables were reached from the [School of Computing directory](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/about/directory/)'s CS faculty link; the 2022–26 [CS people page](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/cs/people/) embeds the faculty rows in page source. Both parsers keep printed names, titles and profile URLs while dropping contact details. For the embedded rows, each name and title quote is checked against the saved raw source because the generic HTML text extractor omits scripts.

The 19 accepted half-year captures yield 2,536 dated observations and 336 distinct listed names. All sampled 2016, 2019, 2021 and 2024 entries had verifiable source quotes; the full row build recorded no missing quotes. The capture plan has zero unplanned slot-halves. It records no archive capture for 2018 H2, 2019 H1 and 2022 H2. The other half of each of those years has an accepted capture; no neighboring capture was substituted.

The raw capture receipts and plan are in `listings/nus/raw/captures.sqlite`; parsed rows and coverage are in `listings/nus/derived/`. `stage_gate.evaluate("nus", 1)` passes with zero open findings, zero waivers and zero reviewed gap years. This is source coverage, not yet a Stage 2 decision that all listed roles are supported PIs.
