# Exact-work student authorship from a university identifier

An optional Cambridge `reviewed/student-authorship-review.json` certifies one
retained member on one already accepted lab paper. It leaves all ordinary
name-only/source-record guards intact. It admits no student or paper, changes no
name, school, role, date, source observation or adviser relationship, and supplies
no collection/OpenAlex/global-person identity anchor.

A fresh independent reviewer must replay original source bytes and exact quotes.
The original PhD supervisor directory must bind the named student, source record
and its owned university profile or explicitly agreeing thesis identifiers to the specific CRSid. A captured university policy
must explicitly support the literal `[email removed]` mailbox convention. Version
1 supports that domain only; departmental and other email aliases are not inferred.

The complete primary PDF must establish its exact DOI, title, publication date,
complete ordered author list, and the member's unambiguous named author slot,
literal university mailbox and explicit University of Cambridge affiliation.
An address in references, acknowledgements or another person's correspondence
proves nothing. The source bytes, complete text hash, parser result and page count
are retained. A publisher DOI or OA cluster's author ORCID alone is insufficient.
Version1 holds nonempty edition groups until their authorships have separate proof.

The stated enrollment/end or current-capture interval establishes the person's
Cambridge PhD student interval, never a PI advising start. Publication dates at
stated precision must fit that interval. The separately labelled historical
student route implements the existing six-year post-degree link window; it
claims former-student authorship, never publication-time enrollment. Future,
undated, competing and uncertain identities or times remain held.

The decision binds every scientific member, accepted-paper and PI field, except
explicit AtlasID augmentation and build summary counters. A new native source,
member relation, paper metadata/edition or PI claim makes the proof stale.
Duplicate decisions never use first-row wins. Existing links remain unchanged.

A certified link carries `institutionalAuthorshipReview.identity_scope=exact_work`.
Both AtlasIDs byline bridge paths exclude these proofs through the common
`work_only` predicate. Complete pre-ID and post-ID validation rechecks the
scientific preimages and typed proof. This does not repair or authorize any old
global join. The absent optional input preserves ordinary behavior.

The consumer runs after ordinary weak-member filtering and honors active
student-link exclusions. Its input limit is 5 MiB. It records every applied or
held decision in `report.json` as `studentAuthorshipReview`. Scientific
preimages also bind the complete native work source and its ordered byline;
competing native versions are held.

Original directory HTML, university mail-policy HTML and primary PDF bytes must
be available in the private content-addressed `raw/source-evidence/<sha256>.bin`
cache. Input rows cannot choose filesystem paths. Consumption rehashes original
bytes, replays the source-owned directory entry and PDF text, rejects duplicate
native IDs, and checks quotes against the actual publication header. Neither raw
source bodies nor that private cache are committed with reviewed inputs.

A separately labelled `recorded_student_publication_window` implements
R-LNK-01/03's two years before to six after retained student years. Version1
requires those years to belong to this exact original PhD directory relation
and agree with its stated end/current observation year. It derives both bounds
from the bound native member, never accepts input-supplied window bounds, and
requires complete native and primary publication-date intervals inside them.
It certifies authorship by a recorded student; it makes no publication-time
enrollment, degree-end or PI advising-start claim. Title footnote markers may
trail a complete title; words, title substrings and unrelated editions may not.

A genuine publisher month-year assertion is represented as `YYYY-MM`, never an
invented first day of the month. Native and primary publication assertions must
have the same publication year; both complete date intervals must fit the
chosen permitted window. Received and accepted dates are not publication dates.
Named email tokens and DOI tokens are replayed from their complete occurrences
in the actual original text, so clipped quotes cannot certify a larger token.

The named-address mapping in Version1 accepts only one complete, semicolon-delimited
selected-author clause within the actual publisher `Authors’ addresses` or
`Authors’ Contact Information` block. It must contain exactly the selected email
address (all domains counted), with no other author's name. A clipped column or
partial clause is held; other address layouts require a separate proof form.

Additional closed forms preserve the same exact-work scope. An explicitly named
printed-name form may omit middle names, add compatible middle names/initials,
restore Latin diacritics, omit a second surname under the existing name rule,
or use one edit in a full given name. Every form still requires the literal
owned university account. Conflicting full given names and initials remain held.
A one-word native acronym can explicitly map to its complete primary subtitle
only with the same unique actual publisher DOI; native paper metadata stays intact.

A same-author `; e-mail:` continuation belongs to its preceding whole address
clause. Numbered affiliations require actual byline markers, separate literal
institution definitions and a complete alternating name/email correspondence
block. Same-row author columns require both the complete literal header and
original PDF word coordinates reparsed with `pdftotext -bbox`. Column boundaries
come from the complete author-name row, without input-supplied rectangles;
straddling words, overlapping authors and unsupported vertical layouts are held.
A publisher download cover permits article page2 only when the original cover
separately establishes the exact full title, DOI and ACM publisher.

A directory entry without an owned profile may use `owned_thesis_identifiers`
only when its own bold name, unique `s-CRSid`, official Apollo
`pubs.staff-id:CRSid` query and official local `CRSid-thesis.pdf` all agree.
It supplies no invented profile, external identity anchor or global merge.

The separate `university_thesis_author_orcid` form replays an original Apollo
item's own structured URL and unique author DOM slot: the full thesis author
name and ORCID anchor must belong to that same person loader. The original
Date, Advisors, PhD Qualification and University of Cambridge Awarding Institution
fields must agree with the retained native source, role and recorded year.
The standard two-before/six-after window uses that recorded thesis year; it
asserts neither an enrollment interval nor an advising start.

Its primary authorship proof is an actual ORCID URI annotation belonging to the
selected printed author's byline name, reparsed from the original PDF with
PyMuPDF. A badge must immediately follow that complete name, overlap its row,
have bounded dimensions and intersect no competing author's name. The separately
typed whole-name link must have the complete name's exact original rectangle.
An OA author-cluster ORCID cannot supply either proof. Printed given initials
are permitted only in this exact original-ORCID form and must agree with all
retained full given names; they never become a name-only alias or global anchor.

Affiliations replay one complete shared unindexed address block, original
numbered definition rows, whole named publisher-address groups, whole explicit
"is/are with" publisher paragraphs, or original same-row author coordinates.
Shared unindexed proof rejects competing blocks even when their employer name
contains no university keyword. Numbered definitions and complete actual numeric
author-marker tails are replayed independently of input quotes, preventing a
clipped comma from dropping a second affiliation. A separately typed numbered
correspondence footnote adds no institution. Multiple university advisers must
replay the entire original list and select an exact named adviser slot.

Titles interleaved with publisher sidebars can use a whole original PDF title
block before the author row. The primary DOI must be the unique pre-reference
work identifier and belong to the actual header, a complete formal identifier
footer line, or a same-column ACM copyright/year/publisher imprint. A DOI in
body discussion or references alone is insufficient. These closed forms use the
same exact-work marker and both global-ID bridge exclusions. Existing scientific
source observations and accepted paper metadata remain unchanged.
